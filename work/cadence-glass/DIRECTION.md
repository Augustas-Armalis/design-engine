# Cadence glass: dark hero (own design)

scope: hero only, one 1440x900 fold, still screenshots (no animation)
motion: none
mode: A (scratch) with C (references: Kargul Chatsheet hero, Linear fold, Apple liquid glass)
brief (founder 2026-10-21): "cook something of your own, preferably in dark mode with purple glows and liquid glass like something cool af to get a lot of views"

- **Hero class:** product proof with one signature object.
- **Concept:** Cadence arranges your week. The fold is a night sky with one purple eclipse rising over the horizon. A liquid-glass command surface floats in front of it and bends the light: the visitor sees the product thinking.
- **Reference triad:**
  - Structure: Kargul Chatsheet, with quiet centred copy over one loud object that carries the brand mark.
  - Identity: Linear, with near-white h1 at 500, graphite canvas and alpha text ladder.
  - Material: Apple liquid glass. Real refraction (SVG displacement on backdrop), specular rim, no frosted-grey blur slab.
- **Owned decisions (3):**
  1. The purple eclipse light field.
  2. The liquid-glass command bar with three slot answers.
  3. The Cadence four-tile mark lit from inside.

overrides:
- override: dark "no glows" (founder-taste dark rules) because the founder asked for purple glows in this brief. The glow is one rendered light source (the eclipse) behind the glass, not blobs behind the type, and resting buttons stay unglowed.
- override: colour.colourful-pixels because the founder's brief asks for purple glows (2026-10-21); the colour is one rendered eclipse light behind the glass, not UI colour
