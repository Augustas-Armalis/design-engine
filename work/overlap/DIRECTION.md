# Overlap: direction

**Brief (founder, 2026-10-19):** "a stunning design for me to post... a card of some component, and a hero, something clean and good af." Scope: one component card (post image) and one hero. Nothing else.

**One line:** Overlap is scheduling for distributed teams. It finds the hour everyone is awake. Audience: remote team leads. Action: Start free. Proof: the product computing the overlap across real time zones (sample data, labelled).

## Hero class
Product proof as data-viz (light-hero-grammars "data-viz as hero: one timeline across the fold"). The product's own verb, finding the overlap, is the visual: a 24-hour timeline of six teammates with the shared hour lit in blue.

## Concept
The world is a 24-hour day seen from six cities at once: the visitor reads where the work hours stack up.

## Reference triad
- Structure: editorial top band (headline left, subtitle and CTAs right, bottoms aligned) over a full-width product band. Taken from the hero-craft study: the product ends the hero after the largest gap (HC-06), at 18 / 32 / ~96.
- Type: Weekline's owned decision. Regular-weight Host Grotesk display at large size (56/450, -0.035em), with Geist Mono for times.
- Proof/material: Quorum's product detail level (Kargul density, sample-data label, one accent only on meaning).

## Bound system
weekline (calendar/scheduling). Kept: regular-weight display, blue only for what the product proposes, product window as the light source.

## Overrides
- override: weekline dark-first because the brief is "clean" and the last build (Kite) was dark; the light ladder is local to `index.css` and theme.css is not imported.
- override: variety pick (layout=split-bleed nav=G13 button=compact type=mono-terminal system=vigil) because split-bleed repeats Quorum, G13's colour band fights the one-accent rule, and mono-terminal/vigil are security voices that do not fit scheduling.
