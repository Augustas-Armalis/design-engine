Create more than you Consume
This is a mistake you must avoid if you want to understand 
UI design. It's thousand times easier to read random design 
papers, keep scrolling social media for inspiration than it is 
to design.

I know it’s important for designers to consume the latest 
information and knowledge in their field but it’s necessary 
to maintain a balance between them.

More creation leads to more practice and ultimately help 
you to enhance your skill in UI design.
