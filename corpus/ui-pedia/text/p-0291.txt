Hover - indicates when a user's cursor is hovering
over an interactive element.
Active - or pressed state indicates that the button
has been pressed by the user.
Progress/Loading - When an action is not
completed immediately, loading is used to signal
that the component is in the process of completing
the action.
Disabled - indicates that the component is now
disabled, but that it may be enabled in the future.
