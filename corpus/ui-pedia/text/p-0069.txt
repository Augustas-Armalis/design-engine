Get inspired from the Art and Nature
Color inspiration for Ul concepts can be found in nature and
art, which you can take advantage of. Take a look at the
gross, for example, and notice the vorious colors of green.
People choose colors that appear to be notural.
They appear to be user-friendly. Art can be thought of as a
direct mirror of the notural world. As a result, you can utilize
it as a source of inspiration as well. The Evian brand logo, for
example, incorporates not just natural hues but also natural
design in its logo.
evian
By this, it should've been clear to you how important color is
in Ul design. While choosing the color scheme for a brand,
never choose just any random color. Every color depicts
something. And that depiction should be the same as your
brands. The best User Interface Design is one in which each
color is carefully picked.
