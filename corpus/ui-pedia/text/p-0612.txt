creative authority.) Maybe you're working on a side project
with a partner. If your partner has a lot of feedback on the
design, you have two tasks: (1) understand your partner's
feedback on the design, and (2) strive toward something
that both of you ore happy with. This is much more difficult -
especially for o beginner designer - than simply trying to
create something you enjoy. So I strongly advise you to look
for a project where the criterion for success is whether or not
