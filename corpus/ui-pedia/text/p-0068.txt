Simplicity
The user experience is improved by using simple color
combinotions. Half of a designer's job is done if the human
mind considers an application easy to look at. A simple
color scheme with two contrasting hues or a
monochromatic color scheme is less distracting to the eye
and makes it easier to assimilate material.
Having too many colors in too mony locations, on the other
hond, generates a convoluted interface that confuses the
user.
