7. Focal Point
According to the focal point concept, whotever visually
stands out will capture and hold the viewer's attention first.
When you look at the image bove, for exomple, the first
thing you notice is the red square, which stands out among
the black circles. It's the first point of interest that cotches
your eye, and then your focus shifts to other areas of the
image. For e.g.The focus point principle is used by Twilio to
bring your ottention to their call-to-action button.
