A 2-column layout is naturally created when you divide a
golden rectangle into a square and a smaller golden
rectangle. The resulting layout is perfect for a main content
area and sidebar. Loosely following this layout creates
structure and balance to an interface. However, on today's
responsive websites, it will be hard to fully support the
golden rotio across all viewport sizes. If you focus on
creating a basic grid based on the ratio and align
components to it, you will be able to get the basic benefits
of the ratio at least on some screen sizes.
