Use tabular typography
For numerical numbers, a monospace typeface is strongly
recommended. Because all characters have the same size
instead of having proportional spacing (where "W" is wider
than "I" or "9" is broader than "I"'), this form of typography is
more easier to read and compare. This avoids issues like
