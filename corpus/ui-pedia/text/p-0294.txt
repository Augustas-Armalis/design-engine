Don't Make me think
Steve Krug, a usability engineer, has written a book called
Don't Make Me Think. One of the numerous points it makes
is the need of making the interface plain to users rather
than creating puzzles or mazes. We have a specific
expectotion of how buttons should look and work based on
years of using various devices and other things.
Users will experience delays and confusion if there is a
significant departure from what is deemed "normal."
