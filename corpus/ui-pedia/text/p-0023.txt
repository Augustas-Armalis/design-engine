Ul Design
Let's start with the regular Wikipedia definition of the UI
Design - "User Interface (UI) is the design of user interfaces
for machines and software. The goal of Ul design is to make
the user's interaction as simple and efficient as possible
through aesthetic means".
In simple words, User Experience design is all about the user
experiencing the product, and having a delightful
interaction with it. User Interface design is its visual kind of
cousin so to speak. It's all about the look, feel, Interaction
and the presentation of the product itself. At some point,
some of you might think - doesn't that help me have a
better User Experience? Well, it does. And th1t's why there
are some cross over, some gray areas in to this. But Ul is
more closely relted to the visual communication, or it's like
a graphic design as in picking colors and typography, and
making things really beautiful and statistically pleasing.
