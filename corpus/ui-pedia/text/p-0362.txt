Don't (EVER) use center alignment
It's crucial to employ the proper alignment for the right type
of text if you want to improve the user's reading, mental
calculations, and row comparisons. The eye jumps about
excessively as a result of center alignment, which limits
quick scanning ond identifying abnormalities.
Avoid duplication
Avoid repeating the headline in every cell of a column if at
all possible. For example, in cells like "Qualified Lead" or
"Nurturing Lead," you can skip repeting the word "lead."
Visual noise can be reduced by using the word "Lead" in the
headline ond only using qualifiers in the rows.
