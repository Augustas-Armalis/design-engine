What is a Form?
A form is a set of questions that employ field entries to
collect information. With that said, there are many different
types of forms and roles that each one serves.
Some forms, such as login or checkout forms, are short and
serve as a basic means to an end. Others, such as customer
feedback surveys or event registrotion forms, deal with
complex issues and require more questions to collect all of
the essential data.
