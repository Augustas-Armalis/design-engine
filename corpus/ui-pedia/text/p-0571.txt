Point to be noted
Remember that there are a variety of ways to incorporate
blurs and shadows in your design, or even both. Experiment
with the settings to see how they effect the overall mood of
your creation. An interface can become more entertaining
by switching o thin, dork shadow for a colored, diffuse one.
Your users' ability to concentrate on o particular element
may be improved by blurring the background.
Also, below are some points that you should keep in mind
while using shadow in your design. So take a cup of your
favorite coffee nd follow these 6 simple steps for stunning
shadows.
1. Do not use shadow defaults
It makes no difference whether you employ Sketch, Figma,
or Adobe XD. All of the design tools' default shadow
presentations are terrible. Use them not! You must
constantly alter their appearance if you want to make them
appear sleek and contemporary.
