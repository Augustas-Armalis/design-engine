We are hardwired to want immediate gratification. And,
while it's easy to overlook microinteractions in the grand
scheme of things, they're crucial for keeping your users
engaged. As they say, the devil is in the details. Toggling
between screens, emphasising a feature, or flashing a new
notice are just a few examples of small interactions and
design features that may make a big difference in
improving a user's experience.
How to design Microinteraction?
Micro-interactions are fascinating for designers because
they allow them to try out new design ideas and find fresh
ways to surprise users. However, there ore a few factors to
keep in mind when doing so:
• Put yourself in the shoes of the user nd use all of your
