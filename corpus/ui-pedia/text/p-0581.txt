4. Problem-solving skills
Design is about resolving issues ond making things run more
smoothly and easily for end users. After a sequence of tries
and errors, a designer's task is to choose the optimal
solution. After comprehending the precise brief supplied by
the client and working on the constructive comments
provided on the design prototype, the difficulties must be
ddressed from all perspectives.
Problems are bound to arise no matter what project you're
working on, especially when you least expect them. Great
problem-solving abilities aid in remaining calm and
overcoming obstacles.
