Letters may need to be resized to fit a certain space. There
are three distinct ways to move type around in a space to
improve its appearance. Kerning, tracking, and leading are
the terms for the below given three metrics.

The space between two individual letters is known as
kerning. It's utilised when only one letter has to be moved
because it's too far away or too close to its neighbours. The
space between the capital letter and the rest of the word
seems odd in some typefaces.
