Mentioning PPI - PPI or Pixels per inch refers to the display
resolution or pixel density of a digital device.
PPI will help you determine the resolution of your phone
screen or your computer. The greater the PPI, the greater the
detail in the image or display. On the contrary, a lower PPI
will result in a lower resolution.
