What is the secret to using gradients as o package
backdrop element? Ensure that it complements your brand.
Something bright and risky (like a neon gradient picture
overlay or a loud colour palette) can work well if you're
producing packaging for a direct new product aimed
towards extreme sports fans. A single colour gradient or a
palette that makes use of postel tones is generally a better
option if you're trying for a more delicote ond understated
vibe, like for a new face wash.
