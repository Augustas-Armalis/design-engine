The little gap between design elements is referred to as
micro white space. It's located between lines and
paragraphs. It comprises the space between grid pictures
as well as the space between menu links.
The amount of micro white space in a piece of content has a
direct impact on its legibility. Marginal white space around
paragraphs, for example, has an impact on the user's
reading speed and understanding.
People read more slowly when content oppears in margins
outside of regular paragraphs. They find it more difficult to
comprehend than text with no margins.
