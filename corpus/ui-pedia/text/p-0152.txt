It's always a good idea to have your own Ul pattern library
to use as a style guide for your projects-and it's a lot easier
than you think. Consider the following three best practises
while establishing your own Ul pottern collection.
Familiarize yourself with popular Ul pattern
librories: Looking through Ul design libraries will
give you a decent notion of which patterns to use
and which ones to avoid.
Develop a clear hierarchy: It may seem strange to
conceive bout utilising patterns to organise a
library of Ul patterns, but it's exactly what you'll
need. A sign-in register, for example, should be
distinguished from more involved macro-potterns in
your Ul pattern hierarchy.
Make sure the library is usable: A Ul pottern library
is meant to be used on a daily basis, not to appear
lovely or be confusing to newcomers to the project.
Make sure the text is simple to understand for all
parties involved and does not contain too much
technical jargon.R
