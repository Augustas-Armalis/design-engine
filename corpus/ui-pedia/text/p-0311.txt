Contained or raised buttons are rectangular in design and
use a drop shadow to lift them from the screen surface. This
shadow indicates that the button can be pressed or clicked.
These buttons are mostly used to provide dimension to flat
layouts and to provide usefulness in lorge, busy, or crowded
areas.
