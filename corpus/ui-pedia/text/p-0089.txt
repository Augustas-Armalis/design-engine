Types of Alignment:
1. Vertical Alignment
Vertical alignment is achieved when the centre, top, and/or
bottom of items align on an unseen horizontal line.
Vertical alignment gets its name from the vertical space
within the piece thot is oligned with nother, not from the
invisible horizontal lines. When drawing a line from top to
bottom, one key to remember is to think of the direction
you're going.
A row of pieces need not always be vertically oligned by
their tops, bottoms, and centre. Your elements will have
variable heights in many circumstances, and you'll have to
choose which one makes the most sense.
