Apply Constrints for Responsive Designs
You can use constrints to govern how any element resizes
in reference to its parent frame. You can pin an element
(such as a button) to the corner of a frame, for example, and
keep the precise size and padding when the frame is
expanded or contracted.
Constraints will be relative to their nearest column when
used with a stretch loyout grid to keep a fixed spacing
(gutter) between items. This is particulorly helpful when
developing for a variety of device sizes.
