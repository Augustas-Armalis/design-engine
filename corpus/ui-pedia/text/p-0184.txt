Grids may appear inflexible (or mysterious) to those who
haven't seen them before, but think of them as a blonk
canvas for drawing your icons. They lay out clear guidelines
for keeping your graphic elements in sync. You'll spend less
time ottempting to make sure your icons all work together
and make fewer mistakes if you use grids.

Padding
Final Icon
Padding will be dealt with within your container and grid.
Padding is the space between the icon and the container's
border. It should be tweaked for each icon to ensure that
they all look the same.
