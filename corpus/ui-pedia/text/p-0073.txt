The perceived weight of an element in your design is known
as visual weight. It's a metric for how distinctive an element
is in comparison to its surroundings.
Factors of visul weight
The size, color, contrast, and/or density of an element can
all affect its visual weight.
Let's look at how each of these elements affects visual
weight, assuming everything else is equal.
