3. Validation is non-nigotiable
The form Ul must include some form of validation,
regardless of the type of form you're building. You should
notify users as soon as possible if the data they entered
cannot be accepted; ideally, you should also inform them of
the reason why the data is incorrect.
This is because no one enjoys thinking they've completed a
task only to discover at the last minute that they need to
return. This unexpected awakening might be aggravating
and lead to the user throwing in the towel. Instead, reassure
people that everything is fine - and when something goes
wrong, explain how to remedy it.
