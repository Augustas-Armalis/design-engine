There are a variety of typographic styles that can help you
convey your message more effectively. Itolic and bold are
the most frequent typeface styles. In any graphic design
editor, regular typefaces can be mode bold or italic.
Headlines and highlighting crucial greas of text benefit from
bold styles. The use of bold styles for links makes them out
and is easier to see. Italics are mainly used for words that
are written in a different language or for reference links.
Typeface Families
Some typefaces provide more alternatives than others. A
typeface family might contain up to 20 different styles.
Futura is a typeface family with 22 different styles. Light,
condensed, semi-bold, extra bold, bold condensed, and
book italic are some of the most notable Futura styles.
