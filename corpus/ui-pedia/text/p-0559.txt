Spread
The distance a shadow extends (or contracts) from the edge
of the object it is applied to is known as its spread. From the
element, a spread with a plus number (1 to infinity) expands
outward. A spread that is negative (from -1 to infinity) pulls
an element's edge spread inward.
Blur
The blur of a shadow indicates how much the shadow's
edge is... blurred. You will get a sharp edge with a blur of 0, a
blur of 10 will blur 10 pixels in and out from the shadow's
edge, and so on. The shadow will get lorger and more
diffuse the higher the number.
