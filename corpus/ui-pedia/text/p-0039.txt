Warm Colors
Red color is an example of worm colors. Red, orange,
and yellow are the colors in question. Warmth and
comfort are the sentiments evoked by these colors.
To some people, though, they can also imply rage,
animosity, or fury.
Coca-Cola is a well-known brand that employs red
as its primary company color to evoke feelings of
comfort and warmth.
