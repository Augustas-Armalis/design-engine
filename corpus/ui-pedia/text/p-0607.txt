Let's go over them one by one:
• Logo - I'm not a logo designer, and I don't put on a show
for my clients pretending to be one. So for this first-
iteration style tile, all I wanted to do was make a simple
text logo (or "wordmark" in designer speak). After
experimenting with approximately o third of a dozen
typeface and style alternatives, I settled on Museo bold,
a quirky sons serit that, when small enough, resembles a
serif. For beginners, it'll suffice!
• Colors - Blue is a natural option for any bank or financial
firm. It's the dull, predictable, and unsurprising colour. So
I wanted to use ANYTHING BUT BLUE. Take a look at the
colours on the 1bove-mentioned style tile. Do they refer
to you as "financial" or "money"? If that's the case, it's
because they're the same colours as the euro bills!
Simple idea, seemed worth exploring, so I added it to a
style tile.
