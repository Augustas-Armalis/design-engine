Below are some techniques to improve your navigation
design, which is a make-or-break part of Ul design:
1. Content First
Before sketching a single wirefrome, create the content. The
navigation structure should shape your message to the
world, not the other way around. Websites that are
innovotive, combine cretive concepts with a useful
purpose. Even the best-designed navigation won't keep you
on the page if the content isn't good. Create navigotion to
direct the user experience fter the content has been
created.
Navigation supports Content
• The Menu - It is the first place customers look when they
want to know what's on the site.
• Breadcrumbs - Similar to chapter titles in a book,
breadcrumbs provide context for a situation. Because
many users do not want to arrive at the homepage,
orienting is critical.
• Filters - Filters can help manage information-heavy
websites.
