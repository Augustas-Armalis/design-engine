There's a fascinating narrative behind how Google came to
be in this position. One of the first websites to make great
use of white space was Google.com. Internet connections
were far slower when they first started. Despite the fact that
everything was already on screen, users in user tests would
wait for the page to download. They hadn't expected to see
so much white space! Based on the results of these user
tests, Google designers opted to place a copyright notice at
the bottom of the page to let people know that the page
has fully loaded.
