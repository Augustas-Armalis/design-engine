3. An1lysis
Make advantage of the information gathered during the
research phase in this phase. Create hypothetical personas
and experience maps using the informotion you've
gathered.
• Hypothetical Personas: Creating hypothetical scenarios
allows designers to learn more about the many types of
people who will use your product. It allows for an
accurate portrayal of the final product to be depicted.
After delivery, the design team can work out how it will
look.
• User Experience Maps: A user experience map depicts
the flow of users through your final product. All of this is
accomplished through the use of visual representations
and correct interactions with clients during the product
definition phase.
