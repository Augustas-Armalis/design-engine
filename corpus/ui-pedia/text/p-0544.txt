Adding with light and shadows
Shadows and highlights are all types of Ul gradients,
however novice designers might not be aware of this. The
truth is that every color may have depth by properly
manipulating light and shadow; there is no such thing as a
completely "flat" color.
Perhaps it's time to start experimenting with shadows to add
depth back into your designs now thot the flot Ul colors
design trend is progressively losing favor with some
powerful designers.
