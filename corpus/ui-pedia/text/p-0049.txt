secondary colours, and tertiory colours. Let's understand it...
Primary colors ore those that cannot be produced by mixing
two or more other colors. Red, blue, and yellow are the main
colors.
The secondary colors ore oronge, purple, and green, which
may be created by mixing any two of the three bosic colors.
Tertiary colors are created when you combine a main and a
