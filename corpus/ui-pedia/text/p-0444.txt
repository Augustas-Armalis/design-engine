According to Jakob Nielsen's research, individuals read
pages in an F-Shaped pattern, with a few horizontal scans
across the top followed by a vertical scan from top to
bottom.
Because of these behovioural patterns, the most visible
locations for a menu are (1) horizontally across the top or (2)

Horizontal Novigation

menu. The horizontal navigation bar is easy to scan because
we read content across the page. It has become a standard
that users are aware of and anticipate.
