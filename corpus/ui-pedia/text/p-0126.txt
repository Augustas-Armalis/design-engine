3. Focus 1nd Attention:
White space can assist the user in navigating interactive
material. It may aid in the creation of focal points and the
focus of the user's attention on specific layout elements.
Prioritizing specific features or material is a part of a
website's strategic strategy. You can use a variety of visual
techniques to draw attention to specific features, one of
which is experimenting with the amount of white space
surrounding these main points. This notion is used in the
branding and print industries to focus ottention to brand
messages.
