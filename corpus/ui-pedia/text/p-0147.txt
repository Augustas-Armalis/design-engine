The most common Ul design patterns?
The following re some of the most common user interface
design patterns:
Social Sharing: These patterns make it easier to
promote and share content cross different social
media networks.
Navigation: Navigational patterns assist users in
navigating the Ul and ensure that they may return
to the homepage if they become disoriented.
Input/Output: These patterns include o variety of
forms that allow users to enter data or receive
feedback from the site in response to a specific
activity.
Content structuring: these patterns are concerned
with how your site is organized in a way that feels
intuitive and accessible.
