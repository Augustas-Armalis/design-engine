The figure above depicts the differences between text with
no white space, passive white space, and both passive and
active white spaces:
• The text on the left side of the image has very little
white space between characters, rows, paragraphs,
and so on. We immediately observe that the text is
crushed ond compressed due to the obsence of
white space. It's quite difficult to read.
> We've included some passive (and micro) white
space in the middle example. Do you notice how
much easier it is to read this one than the lost one?
> In the case of the right-hand example, we've used
active white space to help us navigate the text.
Thankfully, the hard "wall of text" we first saw has
evolved into something more familiar. White space
may improve the text's overall readability and flow,
but it's also necessary for our eyes and sanity!
