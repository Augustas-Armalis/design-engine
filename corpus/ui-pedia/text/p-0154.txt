Perception
Our vision allows us to process informotion from the
environment around us, which is tronsmitted to our eyes via
light. The process of our senses gathering up and conveying
information from the world around us is known as sensation.
The process through which the brain organizes and
interprets dota received from sense organs - in the case of
visual perception, the eyes - is known as perception.
Culture, post experiences, and our expectations all
influence how we perceive things.
Seeing is not believing
What should designers be aware of when it comes to
perception? It's helpful to understand some of the processes
thot influence how people experience and perceive our
designs when we create for them. Perception is a personal
and complex process, and we will never be able to fully
control how people perceive things. However, we can
improve how we design to accommodte for some of the
psychological bases of perception.
