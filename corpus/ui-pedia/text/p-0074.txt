Visual Weight by Color
Visual weight is influenced by color in a less evident way.
Our perception of color is influenced by the colors in our
environment. The red square in the illustration below
commonds our attention, giving it greater visual weight
than the yellow square. This isn't always the case, as we'll
see in the next example.
Visual Weight by Controst
We can show how contrast can substantially impact our
perception of color by utilising the same colored squares as
in the previous example. The left square's contrast is
reduced by a darker red background, while the right
square's contrast is increased. As a result the focus is
essentially shifted to the yellow square.
