Importance of animation in Ul design
Users have come to expect powerful, functional, and unique
interfaces that are easy to navigate as the number of
websites and apps grows tenfold every day. Human-
centered, intuitive, and responsive digital products are
required in today's market. Animotion is one of the ways
that designers accomplish all of the above (and more).
By recreating the sensation of engaging with a physical
object in real lite, animation adds a key human touch to
digital interfaces. This makes the user's experience easier
