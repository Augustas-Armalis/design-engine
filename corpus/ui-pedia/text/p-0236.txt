Consider Constraints
Some designers believe that introducing limits to grid
layouts will limit their design origindlity. It's crucial, though,
to recall the old adage, "No order without rules." Creative
restrictions can help you not just see your work from a
different perspective, but also develop better solutions.
They can also assist you in creating a far more original look.
Use a baseline grid to align elements
A baseline grid is a dense grid of horizontal lines that can be
used to align texts, photos, videos, cards, and other objects
on your page. It's a useful tool for creating clear, close
typography and enhancing your design's visual hierarchy.
Place elements inside grid field, not in the gutter
Place items inside a grid field to guarantee that they are all
properly arranged and aligned on the page.
