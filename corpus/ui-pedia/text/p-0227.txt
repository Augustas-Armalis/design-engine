Fluid Behaviour
The container width chonges as the screen size chonges
because fluid layouts are measured in percentages rather
than pixels.
While the column size increases or lowers, the container
width adjusts by keeping the margin and gutter widths
constant.
This behaviour makes use of available screen space,
although items may oppear stretched out as a result.
