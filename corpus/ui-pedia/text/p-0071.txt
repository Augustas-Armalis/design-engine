The term "balance" is used in a variety of situations. Balance
is typically thought to be a very positive thing, whether it's
used to describe your nutrition, the legal system, or standing
on your own two teet. Nobody wants to be out of balance in
anything in the great majority of circumstances.
The same rule applies in the design world. It's important to
strike a good balance.
Objects with the same physical weight will balance each
other on a scale in the physical world. The distribution of
visual weight is referred to as balance in design.
