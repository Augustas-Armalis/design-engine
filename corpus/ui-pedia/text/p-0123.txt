Active vs Passive: Another way to approach
the usefulness of white space
Apart from the distinction between micro and macro white
space, white space can also be classified as active or
passive.
Active Whitespace:
The white space use to improve page structure and lead the
user through the information on the page.
Passive Whitespace:
It is used to improve the layout's attractiveness without
directing the user through a specified reading, flow, or
content order. The white space between font glyphs nd
paragraph lines for example, serves this purpose.
