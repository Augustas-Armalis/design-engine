What is a Ul card?
Cards are Ul elements that serve as content containers.
Cards often have an image, title, description, call to action,
and subheadings or icons. The concept that each card
symbolises one idea, item, or piece of content binds the
cards together. A card is a user interface design pattern
that bundles similar information in a flexible-size container
that visually resembles a playing card, according to Nielsen
Norman Group.
