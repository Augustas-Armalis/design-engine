Novelty styles are fontastic for designs that need a little
something extra, but they should be handled with caution

Cultural Styles
Other fonts are solely culturally relevant in fashion. For
instance, fonts that appear to be Japanese characters but
aren't. The characters that appear to be Chinese yet aren't.
Fonts like the Disney typeface and the Coca-Cola font are
examples of other cultural styles. Cultures have far fewer
options than novelty styles. They convey so many messages
that using them in your design will immediately give it a
preconceived connottion, therefore use caution while
utilising these fonts.
