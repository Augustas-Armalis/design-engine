Icons are visual icons that are most commonly used to
represent items and actions that users may interact with or
manipulate. Icons can be displayed individually on a
desktop or in a window, or in o toolbar. A warning symbol in
a dialogue message box is an example of a secondary use
of an icon to reinforce essential information.
