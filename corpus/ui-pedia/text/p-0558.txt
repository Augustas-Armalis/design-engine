Z-Axis
The third oxis in design is the z-axis, which runs front to back
rother than top to bottom or left to right. You can stack
items using this axis, which means you can utilize your
available space more effectively.
The higher your element is located within the z-axis, the
wider and more diffuse your shadow should be in order to
best mimic real life. By djusting the dispersion and blur of
the shadow, you may control this.
