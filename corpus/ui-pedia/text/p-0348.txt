Curves
A smooth-flowing continuous line that has bent is referred
to as a curve. There are no sharp curves in it. The curve can
be recognised by the fact thot the line bends ond changes
direction at least once.
Two-dimension curved shopes include circles, ellipses,
parabolas, and hyperbolas, as well as arcs, sectors, and
segments. Curves will look great on modern, user-friendly
websites. But use them with caution.
