Breakpoints
Designers and developers can easily adjust the layout of a
design as it goes up from mobile to desktop using
breakpoints. This allows us to adapt our designs to a variety
of device sizes without sacrificing the user experience.
Establishing a set of breakpoints makes it easy to express
how designs should behave across devices of various sizes.
Breakpoints are commonly represented in "px" units ond
occur in ranges. While there isn't a "must-have" set of
breakpoints, here ore several to get you started.
