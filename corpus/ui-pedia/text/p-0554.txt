3. Use gradients wisely
Gradients are useful, as we already stated. They may be
used as an overlay, to provide depth to a flot design, to add
visual interest to a backdrop of a single hue, etc. The list is
endless.
But just because something is adaptable doesn't imply that
you should take it as your new design mantra. Less is
(possibly) more when it comes to gradients, just like any
other trend. Don't complicate the design. Don't go too far.
Gradients are important, but make sure you use them
properly.
4. Spend time on smoothing tr1nsitions

doubtful that you'll succeed in creating one that is

color stops, try adding or removing gradient stages, or
