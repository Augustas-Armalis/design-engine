Person1 Interest
The personal interests of viewers have an impact on how
they perceive design. Some topics pique our curiosity more
than others. If we're more interested in trains than cars, tor
example, the image of a train will grab our attention when
we encounter two items (car ond train). Because of their
various interests, two people viewing the same page can
easily gaze at different objects.
