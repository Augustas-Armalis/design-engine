Tracking
The proportionate gap between all the letters in a piece of
text is known as tracking. Changing the tracking allows you
to fit more letters in a short space or stretch out letters that
are too close together. Due to the way ligatures split and
produce imbalanced spaces, script fonts cannot be tracked
too much.
