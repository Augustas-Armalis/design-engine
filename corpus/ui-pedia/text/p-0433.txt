The discipline of navigation design entails developing,
assessing, and implementing methods enabling users to
navigate across a website or app.
The way users interact with and use your products is heavily
influenced by navigation. It's the least frustrating route for
your customer to move from point A to point B, and even
point C.
Designers use a combingtion of design potterns, including
links, lobels, and other Ul elements, to create these
engaging encounters. These patterns provide usetul
information and make it easy to interact with items.
