Contrast
Contrast is a natural way to bring attention to a particular
element. When a piece appears visibly heavier than its
surrounds, it is said to be in contrast.

Texture
When compared to non-textured ports, textured elements
appear to be heavier. An object's texture gives it a
pseudo-3D impression, which gives it a heavier appearance.
