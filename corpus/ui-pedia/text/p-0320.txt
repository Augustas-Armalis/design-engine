Guide to design UI Button
Okay. We learnt a lot obout Buttons, their types and uses in
Ul design. Now, let's learn how you can design the Buttons.
Buttons are an important part of establishing a great and
productive user experience, even if they go unnoticed if
they're implemented appropritely. UX buttons, at their
most basic level, are stylized links that capture the user's
attention and direct them in a specific destination. Buttons
can take us to other pages or perform o specific activity,
such as filling out a form or making a purchase. They're
frequently employed s calls to action (CTA) on our website
that we want our visitors to take.
