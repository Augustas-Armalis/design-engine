In a nutshell, points and pixels are not the same
thing. Resolutions and pixel densities are a subject
for nother article, so here's a quick rundown:
Points are a vector representation of pixels that
vary in resolution. On current, pixel-dense displays,
one point can be as large as four pixels or more. The
reason for this is that it is necessary to have huge
elements that are also sharp and precise.
