5. Continuity
The continuity principle argues thot elements placed on a
line or curve are regarded to be more linked than elements
not arranged on the line or curve.
The purple dots in the curved line, for example, oppear to be
more reloted to the dark blue dots on the curved line than to
the purple dots on the straight horizontal line in the figure
above.
Because the eye instinctively follows a line or curve,
continuity is a greater indicator of resemblance than color
similarity.
Continuity is used by Amazon to express that the products
below are similar and linked to one another.
