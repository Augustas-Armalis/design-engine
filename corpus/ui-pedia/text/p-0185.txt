While the grid provides a basic fromework for drawing icons,
keylines provide a more detailed direction. The keylines are
a kind of icon-making starter kit that includes a few basic
forms like a square, rectangle, and circle. They assist you in
maintaining consistent aesthetic proportions in your icon
designs while allowing you to express yourself freely.
Another factor to consider when generating visually
consistent icons is the border radius (the amount of sharp or
rounded corners). If one icon hos crisp, shorp corners, you'll
want to make sure they're present in all of your other icons.
Context determines which portions of an icon grid are
required-where the icons will be displayed, how they will be
masked, and which shapes will be used as templates.
