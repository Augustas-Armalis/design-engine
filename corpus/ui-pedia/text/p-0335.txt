known as the outer margin (or simply "margin"). It
enables us to arrange it in a secure manner in
reltion to everything around it. It's the blue space
on the outskirts of our rectangle.
The Inner Margin: The inner margin is also known
as padding, which may be useful when speaking
with developers. It means that the larger the safe
area inside the thing is, the safer it is. It's the darker
area inside our colorful rectangle in the illustration
