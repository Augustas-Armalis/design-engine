2. Set a consistent tone and mood
The general tone or atmosphere of all interfaces is
sometimes established by the typeface style. When you
show the page banners or headlines with a cartoon font
with worm colours, soft lines, and bold strokes, for exomple,
you add fun and personalization to your page. When you
select a plain, simple, and serious typography, though, you
appear to be setting a serious tone for your entire
programme.
