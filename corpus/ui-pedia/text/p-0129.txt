Proximity
Design elements that are close together are considered to
be related, whereas elements that are separated are
considered to belong to different groups.
Gestalt psychologists discovered Gestalt principles in the
first half of the twentieth century while trying to understand
how individuals visually see the environment, specifically
how they decide whether certain elements belong to the
same group or not. These principles include proximity,
similarity, and closure, all of which are important in digital
interface visual design. More grouping principles were later
added to the initial Gestalt list (such as common region).
