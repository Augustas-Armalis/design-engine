4. Common Region
The concept of a common region is strongly linked to
proximity. It states that humans see objects as being
grouped together when they are placed within the same
closed zone.
Adding a border to the elements helps to
create groups if you don't have space to
show the same.
Adding borders or other visual boundaries between
groupings of objects, even if they have the same closeness,
form, color, or other choracteristics, is a fontastic method to
create a perceived distinction.
