Designing Form
Keep things "Short 1nd Simple"
Keep in mind - "The longer your form will be, lower will be
the conversion rote". With each successive question, a
certain percentage of users will give up before reaching the
finish. Users today don't want to wait and don't want to put
up effort in filling out forms, so keep your form design
simple.
Keep only what is absolutely required in your life and let the
rest go. The length of some forms, such as "Sign in" or
"Subscribe to a newsletter" forms, isn't an issue because
they're designed to be brief by def1ult. However, keeping
the form to two or three entry fields isn't always possible.
Thot's because, from the user's perspective, losing the effort
they put in would sabotage the entire experience you had
prepared. At the very least, the users' hord work will not be
lost if the internet connection is broken!
