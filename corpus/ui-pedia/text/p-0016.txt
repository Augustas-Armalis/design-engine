UX Design
So, let's start with User Experience Design. Whot is User
Experience design?
Well, Wikipedia describes UX design something like this -
"User Experience design or say - UX, UXD, UED or XD is the
process of enhancing user satisfaction with a product by
improving the usability, accessibility, and pleasure provided
in the interaction with the product."
Let me break this even more for you guys - User experience
design is, making something enjoyable and usable between
the product (Phone, Website, Table, etc.) nd the user who's
using it. Suppose for example, you're sitting on a chair and
somebody had to put a thought into how it would be a
better experience for you to sit on that chair.
