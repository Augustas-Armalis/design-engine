Angle
Angle determines the clockwise rottion of our object, with
0° being the default. Rotation can be a negative number as
well. It's worth noting that a -15° rotation is equivalent to
360° - 15° = 345°.

It's odvisable not to rotate things by hand in order to
maintain a consistent interface; instead, enter the proper
angle from the keyboard, which is far more precise than
manually turning the item point by point.
If we Flatten the object (there's a button for that), the ongle
is reset to 0, but the object is still rotated - just the new
perspective becomes the new rotation base.
