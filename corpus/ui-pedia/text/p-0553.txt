Examining websites for vehicles targeted for various
audiences is a fantastic way to see this in action. The
website for Aston Martin always displays the automobile
configurators in profile view with a rodial gradient of
grayscale tones, often ranging from white to black from the
rear of the vehicle. With an older, more mature audience in
mind, this highlights the luxury, elegance, and
straightforward brilliance of their automobiles.
In contrast, the Honda Civic website, which targets
millennials and young professionals globally, displays the
automobile in configurator view against a gradient of
vibront colours in the user interface (UI), often yellows and
blues, to represent the brand's youthful and joyful side.
Use complimentary colours as well; while gradients in user
interfaces are a great way to liven up a design, when done
improperly, they oppear unprofessional and uninspired.
Designers may experiment with colour theory and UI
gradients with this tool from Codepen. Before moving on to
find designs, we'd strongly advise testing your concepts.
