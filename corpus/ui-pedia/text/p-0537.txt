What ore Gradients?
The gradual transition from one hue to another is known as
a gradient. It allows the designer to essentially make a
brand-new hue.
It gives designs new dimension and gives the thing more
reality, which helps the product stand out. Gradients
essentially add depth.
A gradient is a progressive transition from one colour to
another (or, if you're feeling porticularly creative, from one
colour to another colour to another colour; gradients aren't
restricted to two shades). Gradients ore also known as
colour transitions.
