What is Button?
A button, as an engaging and interactive element, ollows
users to receive interactive feedback from the system in
response to a specific command. It's a control that allows
consumers to communicate with a digital product directly
and transmits vital commands for achieving a certain goal.
In order to create the correct interactions, we must first
understand the history ond origins of physical pushbuttons,
