In some circumstonces, you'll have to choose between
several icons that all reflect the same idea. Conflicting icons
are what they're called.
To like a post on Focebook, for example, you'd use a thumbs
up, while on Instagram, you'd use a heart, and on other sites,
you'd use a star. While any of those icons can be used in
your design, the key is to be consistent and choose only one
for your interface taht suits the best for it.
