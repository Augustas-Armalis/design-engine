4. Color
To make some buttons easily identifible, you must choose
an appropriate colour. Human behaviour and emotion are
strongly linked to the visual environment, ond colour is a
powerful weapon in this regard.
When choosing colours for buttons, keep in mind that the
background and button colours should contrast well to
distinguish the buttons from other Ul elements.
5. Microcopy
The strong button microcopy is often constant but brief,
allowing it to quickly capture users' ottention. It is
sometimes done in capital letters to make the copy more
appealing in the layout.
This is not necessary, as the decision is based on the font,
basic design concept, and mood of the text message.
