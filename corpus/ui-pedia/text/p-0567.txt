Blurs
Blurs were briefly discussed in the elements of a shadow
section, but now we'll go into more detail bout what they
are and how to apply them. In contrast to shadows, which
must be applied from an element's edge, blurs can be
applied directly to an element.
Types of Blur
Depending on whether you're working on a graphic or an
interface, there are many blur kinds you might employ. We'll
go over the ones you can apply to interfoce design, but if
you're interested in learning more about blurs, check out the
gaussian, motion, and radial blur types online!
Background
Only the element's background receives a background blur
rather than to the actual element. Imagine you have a
square with a picture as the background. The image would
appear blurred if you applied a background blur, but the
edge of the element would still be razor-sharp.
