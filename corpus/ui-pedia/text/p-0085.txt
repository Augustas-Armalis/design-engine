In Ul design, you are required to keep everything blanced.
And when everything is balanced, hormony is created.
Mointaining a sense of uniformity in your overall design
requires Ul design balance. Visual strain can be caused by a
loss of equilibrium, which should be avoided in most
instances. Visual tension, on the other hand, can be exploited
to accomplish a desired consequence if done correctly.
Take a step back when designing a layout and consider
whether the entire composition feels balanced. You can
experiment with size, color, contrast, and density to help
spread the visual weight if one piece draws too much
attention.
In design, there are four different types of balance as I
already taught you, but it shouldn't stop you from ottempting
to grasp radial balance. Each sort of balance can be
combined to create o more dynomic and lively design.
