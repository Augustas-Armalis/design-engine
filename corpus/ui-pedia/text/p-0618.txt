1. Product Definition
The initial step in the user design process is product
definition. The team in charge of gathering user needs will
do so based on their business context.
It is critical since it is during this phase that the true extent
of the product and its existence is realised.
It's simple: inform your UI/UX designers 1bout the
requirements before beginning work!
The Design Team, Business Manager, and Product Manager
are all octive in this phase. Clients should be consulted by
the entire team in their surroundings. Examine their
requirements within the context of your business.
User Personas, User Stories, and Use Case Diagrams are
important deliverables of this phase.
