With the widespread usage of social media plotforms,
emailing, and chatting, share buttons make it easier to
connect content on o website or app to users' social
networks.
Users can post their material or accomplishments directly
on social hubs with this type of button. These buttons are
labelled with icons to make the link more obvious. These
icons highlight an easily recognisable brand emblem for
certain social media channels.
If sharing is not the user's primary activity on a page, it is
not designated as a button in any way (no colour, extra
shapes, underlining, etc.) - you only see the icons, yet they
are engaging. This method encourages minimalism and
effective use of negative space.
