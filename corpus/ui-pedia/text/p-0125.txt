Three elements to consider when designing
negative space
1. Legibility:
Micro white space, as we've seen, is critical for legibility of
interface material. When selecting design typography
characteristics such as font, size, color, style, leading,
kerning, and tracking, designers should keep white space in
mind. Do you recall the image on the previous page? The
impact of chonging the white space arrangement on
reading performance and the overall user experience is
significant. Readers that are happy are much more inclined
to stay on your page.
2. Design tone and brnding:
The use of white space contributes to the overall design's
tone. Larger mounts of macro white space on a website
may convey minimalism and elegance. Websites with a
smaller quantity of macro white space, such as news sites,
may appear to be more informative (Kyrnin, 2015).
