How can you measure visual weight?
Visual weight is a measure of how much something draws
the viewer's attention, yet unlike physical weight, it cannot
be quantified. However, a simple test may be used to
determine which objects on o page have greater visual
weight than others. Take a few seconds to look at a page or
screen. Close your eyes for a moment. Try to recall which
thing (or objects) from the page are still fresh in your mind.
Those items will be more visually significant than others.
