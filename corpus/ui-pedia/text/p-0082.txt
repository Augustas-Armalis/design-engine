For instance, you can have several small elements that
balance out one large element. Or, you can have smaller
elements positioned further away from the center of the
composition. In either case, the elements are not the same
size and not positioned evenly like with symmetrical
balance. However, your composition still has a sense of
balonce while creating visual interest.
You can see a great example of asymmetrical balance in

It can be more challenging to design in an asymmetrical
way because you can't just perfectly match each side's
visual weight. You will have to experiment with different
ways to balance the elements to give it the right feel.
