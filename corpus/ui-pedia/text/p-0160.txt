Now, we'll go through seven Gestlt laws or principles that

1. Figure-Ground
According to the figure-ground principle, people intuitively
view objects as being in the foreground or background. They
either stand out in the front (the figure) or fade into the
background (the text) (the ground).
You've probably seen examples of this principle floating
around in memes on social media, or as port of logos.
