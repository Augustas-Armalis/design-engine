Points to take away
It's no surprise that Ul cards are one of the most popular
design elements. Ul cards are adaptable and responsive,
user-friendly, and simple to navigate and share. Cards
conserve your users' ottention and allow them to focus on
the content, while also providing a clean and easy-to-
understand user interface.
Almost any type of business can benefit from this design. It
is not, however, a universal solution. If your users will be
working with data where order and search are vital, use a
list or grid style, as Ul cords ore better for exploring
material. In many circumstances, however, cards might be a
comprehensive solution for a web or mobile opplicotion, or
they can reflect a single aspect of it.
