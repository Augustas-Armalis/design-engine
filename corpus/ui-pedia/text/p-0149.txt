How to use Ul design patterns
Ul design patterns can't be directly applied to a user
interface. We'll have to customize them to match a certain
case. So, how do we go bout doing it?
Begin by defining the problem you're trying to solve. Go to
your preferred Ul design pattern library with a clear problem
in mind. Select the category that best meets your
requirements.
Take a look at all of the different samples provided. What is
the intended applicotion of the pattern? What other
designers have done with it? What lessons can you take
away from it? Pay ottention to the many types of elements
and how they are organized.
