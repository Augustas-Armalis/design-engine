Neumorphism
A novel method of skeuomorphic building is known as
"neumorphism." Neumorphism gives the entire design style
1 fresh focus even if it is connected to Skeuomorphism.
Here, the focus is on the color scheme rather than on how
the real ond virtual worlds compare or resemble one
another.
You read that accurately; that is true. The main goal of
neomorphism is to entirely alter the hue of the user's
experience on the screen.
Neumorphism Design
The fundamental tenet of neumorphism is that the
background color must match the color of the elements.
Additionally, to give the impression that the parts are
extensions of the surroundings, the highlighting is done in a
subtle yet effective manner. Each Ul feature is projected
with a light shadow on the upper left corner and a dark
shadow on the bottom right.
