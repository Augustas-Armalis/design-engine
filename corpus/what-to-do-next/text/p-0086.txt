Prepare a (Realistic) Schedule
Our proposals always include a schedule. Timesheets will 
aid in planning, but keep in mind other tasks you're working 
on at the same time to minimize bottlenecks. Above all, 
avoid over-promising!
Don't overlook terms and conditions
If you have a strong proposal, your T&Cs are merely backup 
for those (hopefully) infrequent but unpleasant instances, 
such as when a client pays late or cancels a project halfway 
through. Before you begin, use the T&Cs to establish 
permissions and releases, copyright, and terms of ownership 
for your work.
