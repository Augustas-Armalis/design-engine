Visibility
Do the same in the design world as we are encouraged to be 
good citizens in our local communities. Make yourself known 
by being a visible, active member of design groups.

Attending local meet-ups (Meetup.com) and events is one of 
the finest methods to be noticed and keep current. Find UI 
design conferences and get-togethers where you may mix 
and network with other designers.

Write about your design experiences, join online groups, and 
keep active on social media sites such as Twitter, LinkedIn, 
Instagram, Medium, Facebook, Slack, Dribble, and others.
