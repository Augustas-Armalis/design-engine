Don’t Focus on Grammar
The next tip is that I would recommend you to not to look 
into the grammar while you are speaking. Many of us in our 
own country, we learn grammar first and then start with the 
communication. This is the same method that we’ll required 
to practice the English language. 

Check Your Body Language
This means not crossing your arms, not looking the other 
way all the time, and not fiddling with your desk or pen. 
Keep eye contact with the other individual so they know 
you're paying attention.
