The first two definitions are equally applicable to 
freelancing. You give services to another company under 
the terms of a contract. 

However, the third description highlights one significant 
distinction between freelancing and owning an agency: an 
agency is not a one-person operation. Multiple persons are 
required.

A creative agency is a company made up of several 
employees that offers creative services to other companies. 
Offerings can be restricted to a single service or can cover a 
wide range of services. Marketing, advertising, design, SEO, 
and technical consultation are all common services 
provided by creative companies.

While the day-to-day job may differ based on your agency's 
specialty, the process of establishing an agency in any of 
these fields is generally the same.
