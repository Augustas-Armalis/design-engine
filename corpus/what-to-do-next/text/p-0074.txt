Existing Trademarks - Trademarks can be registered, and 
trademark violation can result in significant legal fees and 
penalties. Make sure a name isn't copyrighted by looking it 
up in the Trademark Electronic Search System.

Incorporation -  You'll probably wish to incorporate your 
company at some point. You won't be able to incorporate a 
business name that is already in use in the state where your 
company is located. It's a good idea to check with your 
state's filing office to see if the name you choose is 
available.

Online identity - At the very least, be sure you can get a 
domain name that contains your company's name. 
However, you should double-check the name's availability 
on any social media platforms you intend to use. Branding 
requires consistency across digital media.
