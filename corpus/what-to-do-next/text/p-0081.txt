Knowing that about 40% of those hours will be spent on the 
day-to-day operations of your firm — seeking clients,

To calculate your minimal hourly rate, divide your survival 
budget by your available billable hours. If you wish to earn 
£30k per year and have £15k in business overheads, you'll 
need to generate at least £45k per year to make ends meet. 
By dividing £45k by the number of billable hours (1,152), you 
can determine that you'll need to charge at least £46.88 per 
hour to meet your goal.
