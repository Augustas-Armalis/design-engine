# Larpo hard constraints (summary for the engine)

One-page checklist. Detailed rules with Why/Check/Source are in `larpo-brand-and-constraints.md` (IDs LBC-xx). All paths under `/Users/augustas/dev/larpo/larpo-knowledge-base/`. "HARD" means `context/constraints.md` lists it under Hard; "soft" is listed under Soft. Do not edit Larpo's brand system from this repo; changes to Larpo canon need the Larpo team's shown-diff approval.

## Which layer are you designing?

| Layer | Binding system |
|---|---|
| **Larpo-owned surface** (larpo.studio, social profiles, captions, bios, bot output, any frame presented as larpo, the caption/frame around a concept post) | Full Larpo system below. |
| **Fictional subject inside a labelled concept** | Not bound by the visual system or lowercase; should get its own type, colour, layout, logo and material. Still bound by the truth boundary. |
| **Work for a real client** | The client's brand. [engine note] Larpo's copy constraints are about larpo's own voice and are not stated to bind client copy; proof rules (approved claims only) apply everywhere. |

## HARD (never break)

1. **Client proof.** Publish only claims and quotes the client has approved. Never turn placeholder material, stock portraits or reference copy into an implied endorsement. Never invent case-study outcomes, performance numbers or endorsements. A logo appears only where the client approved it; a prospect is never displayed.
2. **Public copy is lower case**, acronyms included (`saas`, `ai`, `seo`, `cms`, `b2b`), in page titles, meta titles, bios, site copy, bot output. Only proper names keep capitals (`Framer`, `Webflow`, `LinkedIn`). Settled 2026-08-26 (Rokas Remeika).
3. **Never use em dashes** in larpo copy.
4. **Self-description.** larpo is not a product design studio and never says so. Approved line (verbatim): `a full-stack web partner for saas and ai teams.`
5. **Visual system for larpo-owned surfaces.** geist is the only typeface. Black, white and neutral gray only. No gradients, blobs or decorative glow.
6. **Audience.** Seed to series B saas, ai, fintech and software founders and growth teams. **No DTC, no Shopify Plus** targeting or marketing; larpo does not build on Shopify (on-demand inbound only via Eimantas; ask first). Settled 2026-08-23 (Augustas, Marius Vanagas).
7. **Canonical domain** `https://larpo.studio/`; metadata stays consistent with `context/positioning.md`.
8. **Truth boundary for concepts.** Label fictional material as `concept` or `sample`/`demo data`; never imply a client relationship; no invented customers, logos, testimonials, certifications, security claims or metrics; sample data internally coherent (totals add up).

## Soft (bend only with a stated reason, never on larpo's own surfaces)

- Banned vocabulary: world-class, premium, innovative, immersive, full-service, vague "digital experiences" or "transformative design" claims.
- Motion: short opacity and small vertical entrance transitions only; no blur-fly-in, no decorative scroll effects; buttons never jump upward on hover; respect reduced motion.
- Re-verify time-sensitive claims (social activity, client details, pricing, profile data) before publishing.

## Voice

Direct, calm, compact, product-literate; outcome first, then craft. Short sentences, specific nouns; say what is built, who it is for, what happens next; proof beside the claim. Plain CTAs: `book a call`, `write us a message`. Do: "a complete brand and custom website, for bigger teams and projects". Don't: "we craft transformational digital experiences for visionary founders".

## Visual (larpo-owned)

mark only in navigation (lockup in footer/formal); small, rounded, borderless grouping surfaces; secondary actions on a light neutral background with no border; lucide icons, sparingly; thin black branded scrollbar acceptable; work and proof outweigh oversized headlines; references (mobbin, avero, kargul, kree8) are ingredients, not templates.

## Commercial facts that may appear in copy

Launch page from EUR 3,000; full website from EUR 7,000; after-launch work ongoing. Nothing else is committed (no retainers, no rate card). Based in europe, working globally (no city or country recorded; do not claim one).

## Social (X) captions for larpo concept posts (founder rules, 2026-08-31)

Plain description by default (`hero section concept`, `landing page designs`, `recent identity work`); never name the fictional brand; never write from inside the fiction; no clever angle by default; the word `concept` carries the fictional disclosure; no opinion/ego-claim/question/CTA captions; never claim an outcome the image cannot evidence.

## Founder visual rejections for fictional hero concepts (binding on concept stills)

Oversized generic grotesk headline; lowercase-everything nav/CTAs; reflex all-caps eyebrow; floating pill nav and rounded CTA capsules; plain bright rectangle + arrow CTA; automatic left-copy/right-dashboard; automatic big-copy-above/photo-below; generated image + one floating UI panel; filler cards/labels/charts; decorative dashboards; thin neutral monochrome UI at feed scale; generic dark-neon/glow/orb/grid/grain/shader; mobile/extra sections/interaction QA when one desktop still was requested. See `larpo-hero-system.md` LHS-01..LHS-20 and `seed-verdicts.json`.

## Quick self-check before handing anything to Larpo

1. Which layer is this (publisher, subject, client)?
2. Any capitalised acronym, em dash, banned word or "product design studio" in larpo copy?
3. Any invented number, logo, endorsement, certification or outcome? Is every sample figure labelled and reconciled?
4. Does anything aim at DTC or Shopify buyers?
5. Does a fictional concept name appear in the caption?
6. Did you list which constraints you knowingly departed from (Larpo's handover rule, LAI-46)?
