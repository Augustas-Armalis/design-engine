# Larpo hero system

Provenance tags: **[founder]** = recorded as direct founder/user feedback (`04-user-feedback-ledger.md`, `07-hero-collection-r1-feedback.md`, `ASSESSMENT.md`, `x-post-descriptions/my-thinking.md`); **[team research]** = the team's own doctrine. Source paths are relative to `/Users/augustas/dev/larpo/larpo-knowledge-base/`. Attribution note: the ledger says "user" (the X post notes name Rokas; Larpo's deciders are Augustas, Rokas Remeika, Marius Vanagas). Treated here as founder feedback per the task brief; the source does not name which founder gave each verdict.

## 1. Core ideas

1. A Larpo hero for social work is "an authored campaign frame": identity + meaning + composition, all three. Product proof without art direction is sterile; art direction without a mechanism is decoration; a familiar skeleton with both pasted on still looks generated.
2. **Publisher and subject are different layers.** Larpo owns the caption, surrounding presentation and truth claims; a labelled fictional concept owns the product identity inside the frame. Forcing Larpo's monochrome onto the fictional product produced the rejected Baton hero.
3. The failure was repeatedly **structural**: one grammar (neutral grotesk, 62-83 px headline, 46-52 px edge, logo-left/three-links/action-right nav, rounded filled CTA with arrow, big image + floating proof card, cream/coral/cobalt/amber/dark palette) applied to unrelated brands. Changing colour or photo does not diverge.
4. What the founder accepted (Take, Mise, Borne, Nacre) shares one property: the whole frame belongs to one product world, and restraint is spent with spatial confidence. What was rejected is either a familiar skeleton (Baton, Linecheck) or a forced gesture (Phase, Ridge 47, Vanta).
5. The founder prefers a beautiful still over implementation complexity; interaction, mobile and extra sections are out of scope unless asked.

## 2. Rules

### 2a. Founder-feedback rules (hard guardrails for fictional hero concepts)

- **RULE [LHS-01]** [founder] Do not set the headline as an oversized generic grotesk that dominates most of the frame.
  **Why** Rejected default in the 2026-08-29/30 hero exercises; scale became the idea and crowded the product.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** H1 bounding box < 28% of frame height and the family is not Geist/Inter/Manrope/Space Grotesk-class without a written reason; `visual`. **Source** `inbox/design-generation-refresh-2026-08-30/04-user-feedback-ledger.md` "Rejected defaults".

- **RULE [LHS-02]** [founder] Do not use lowercase-everything branding in a fictional product's navigation and CTAs. Use normal capitalisation, readable size.
  **Why** Rejected default; lowercase-everything is Larpo's own publisher voice, not a subject voice (see LBC-06).
  **Applies to** hero concept stills (subject layer). **Check** share of lowercase-initial nav/button labels < 90%. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`; `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Navigation"; `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s6.

- **RULE [LHS-03]** [founder] No small all-caps eyebrow above the title by reflex. Uppercase and mono utility labels are allowed only for authentic data or machine language.
  **Why** Rejected default; the Tidehouse audit calls it "the exact decorative taxonomy pattern the user identified".
  **Applies to** hero concept stills, landing page. **Check** element above H1 with uppercase + tracking + <= 13px; `visual`. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`; `inbox/anti-ai-design-playbook-2026-08-28/06-tidehouse-audit.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-04]** [founder] No floating pill navigation and no rounded CTA capsules by default.
  **Why** Rejected defaults; nav becomes a product demo instead of information architecture.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills, landing page. **Check** header container radius >= 999px or detached floating bar; CTA radius >= 999px. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`; `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Navigation".

- **RULE [LHS-05]** [founder] No weak or generic CTA styling: a plain bright rectangle plus arrow with no relationship to the product identity is rejected. Give the CTA a subject-specific detail (shape, icon behaviour, motion, material or placement).
  **Why** Rejected default; the Linecheck blue CTA with arrow was named in the rejection assessment.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`; CTA identity element written in `DIRECTION.md`. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`; `linecheck-hero-proof-2026-08-30/ASSESSMENT.md`; `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "CTAs".

- **RULE [LHS-06]** [founder] Do not use the automatic left-copy / right-dashboard layout.
  **Why** Rejected default skeleton; also listed again in the 2026-09-01 divergence note.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** left text column + right product panel in the fold -> flag for justification. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`; `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s4.

- **RULE [LHS-07]** [founder] Do not use the automatic big-copy-above / photo-below layout (giant headline over a wide image or product crop).
  **Why** Rejected default skeleton.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** headline strip above full-width image/product stage -> flag. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-08]** [founder] A nice generated image with one UI panel placed over it, treated as the complete concept, is rejected. Integrate the product UI into the composition; generated imagery is a material, not the concept.
  **Why** Rejected default; Linecheck's industrial photograph + right-side inspection panel "still formed a familiar template".
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`; `ASSESSMENT.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-09]** [founder] Do not add random cards, steps, side labels, charts or visual fragments only to make the frame feel busy.
  **Why** Rejected default.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** every fragment has a named job (removal test, LAI-33). **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-10]** [founder] Product panels must read as a workflow, not a decorative dashboard.
  **Why** Rejected default.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** the panel answers what changed, what the user controls, what happens next (LHS-39). **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-11]** [founder] Thin neutral monochrome UI that disappears at social-feed scale is rejected.
  **Why** Baton's thin neutral UI lost hierarchy at feed size.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** render at 25% and grayscale; focal object still distinct (LHS-47). **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`; `01-recent-concept-lessons.md` "What Baton exposed".

- **RULE [LHS-12]** [founder] No generic dark-neon, glow, orb, grid, grain or shader styling that has no product meaning.
  **Why** Rejected default.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** each atmospheric device has a product-fact sentence (LDP-15). **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-13]** [founder] When the brief is one desktop X screenshot, do not do mobile work, extra sections or elaborate interaction QA.
  **Why** Rejected as scope creep; Baton spent effort on mobile and extra QA although the deliverable was one still.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** scope lock written in `DIRECTION.md`. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md`; `06-design-generation-runbook.md` "Scope lock".

- **RULE [LHS-14]** [founder] Before building, compare the proposed skeleton with the rejected-defaults list (LHS-01..LHS-13). If it matches one, change the structure before changing colours. After rendering ask: "does this look like a designer built a product-specific campaign frame, or like an agent assembled the usual hero ingredients?" and reject internally if the second reading is plausible.
  **Why** The ledger is "a guardrail against relearning the wrong preferences".
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `node tools/ledger.mjs similar`; `visual`. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md` "Operational interpretation".

- **RULE [LHS-15]** [founder] Accepted direction Borne: calm full-bleed photography, restrained navigation, refined serif typography, quiet booking control, coherent premium atmosphere. Accepted direction Nacre: sculptural central product, spacious composition, restrained palette, elegant display typography, information placed around the image rather than inside a generic content block. Preserve their restraint and spatial confidence; do not repeat their skeletons.
  **Why** Founder feedback "accepted as good" on the R1 collection (2026-08-30); positive evidence, not templates.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`. **Source** `inbox/design-generation-refresh-2026-08-30/07-hero-collection-r1-feedback.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-16]** [founder] Avoid the Phase, Ridge 47 and Vanta failure modes: loud poster palette, oversized record graphic and novelty typography ("too experimental for the desired quality"); side rail + giant numeric device + route overlay + ticket graphic ("forced design gestures"); editorial cinema treatment that "did not meet the desired aesthetic" despite a controlled image and palette.
  **Why** Founder rejected these in R1.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`: no forced gesture; novelty type face needs a premise-based reason. **Source** `07-hero-collection-r1-feedback.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-17]** [founder] Explore a wider range of polished default-modern website styles alongside image-first concepts: restrained product sites, monochrome social-platform clarity, Linear-like product hierarchy, refined editorial SaaS, subtle dark software surfaces.
  **Why** Updated preference after R1 (2026-08-30): the founder rejected experimental extremes and asked for polished, modern defaults too.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** a batch contains at least some polished-modern entries, not only experimental structures. **Source** `07-hero-collection-r1-feedback.md` "Updated preference".

- **RULE [LHS-18]** [founder] Prefer a beautiful still to implementation complexity; hero concept exercises may be static. Prioritise screenshot composition, fonts, buttons, imagery and overall atmosphere. Do not add interaction merely to prove the page works.
  **Why** Stated founder preference (2026-08-30).
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** n/a (scope). **Source** `07-hero-collection-r1-feedback.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-19]** [founder] Reject Baton-class output: sterile monochrome product proof with the publisher identity imposed on the fictional subject. Reject Linecheck-class output: industrial photograph + giant headline + right-side inspection panel + familiar blue CTA, because product-specific details do not rescue a familiar skeleton.
  **Why** Founder rejected both; both folders are kept as negative evidence and must not be placed in a positive reference set.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md` "Rejected examples"; `linecheck-hero-proof-2026-08-30/ASSESSMENT.md` ("rejected by the user on 2026-08-30").

- **RULE [LHS-20]** [founder] Take and Mise were "materially better received than Baton and Linecheck". The lesson is not their layout but that brand field, material, interface vocabulary, image, type and action reinforce one premise. Future work must exceed them rather than restyle them.
  **Why** Ledger positive evidence; relative rating, not an unqualified accept.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`; reference PNGs in `inbox/design-generation-refresh-2026-08-30/reference-images/`. **Source** `04-user-feedback-ledger.md` "Positively useful evidence"; `01-recent-concept-lessons.md`.

### 2b. Publisher/subject boundary and delivery contract

- **RULE [LHS-21]** [team research] Larpo's website, captions, profile assets and any frame explicitly presented as Larpo follow Larpo's canonical system (geist, black/white/neutral gray, lowercase). A clearly labelled fictional product concept gets its own type, colour, layout, imagery and logo territory unless the brief explicitly asks for a Larpo-branded concept. The Larpo caption and truth boundary stay Larpo's.
  **Why** The Baton weakness was caused by the preflight applying Larpo's publisher identity to the fictional subject.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `DIRECTION.md` states publisher layer vs subject layer; subject fonts/colours differ from the Larpo set. **Source** `inbox/design-generation-refresh-2026-08-30/README.md` "Diagnosis"; `playbooks/visual-deliverable-preflight.md` Gate 0; `context/constraints.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-22]** [team research] Label fictional names, workflows and sample data as `concept` or `sample`/`demo data`; never present them as client proof. Keep the label legible at feed scale (an in-frame label alone does not survive feed scale; the post copy must also state it is a fictional concept).
  **Why** Truth boundary; the evenbooks sidecar notes the in-frame label does not survive feed scale.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** string "sample" or "concept" in the DOM; caption includes the word `concept`. **Source** `README.md` (refresh) "Truth boundary"; `assets/concepts/2026-08-evenbooks-hero-concept-01.png.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-30]** [team research] For "one desktop hero and screenshot for X" build exactly: one hero, desktop only, 1920 x 1080 composition, 3840 x 2160 lossless PNG export, browser-openable source. An explicit different X ratio from the user wins. Keep critical text and actions clear of a 96 px outer safe area.
  **Why** Locked delivery contract (playbook status active, updated 2026-08-30).
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** viewport 1920x1080; export dimensions 3840x2160; text/CTA bounding boxes >= 96 px from edges. **Source** `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s1; `inbox/design-generation-refresh-2026-08-30/03-x-saas-hero-spec.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-31]** [team research] Deliver three things: the source, the 4K PNG and a browser-openable preview. Binary files over 2 MB do not go in the Larpo KB (use JPEG fallback, e.g. `linecheck-hero-x-4k.jpg`).
  **Why** KB pre-commit blocks binaries over 2 MB.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** file listing. **Source** `03-x-saas-hero-spec.md`; `inbox/linecheck-hero-proof-2026-08-30/README.md`; `AGENTS.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-32]** [team research] Load at most three skills for a hero: `frontend-design` (subject-specific identity and spatial exploration), `product-proof-saas` (believable mechanism and coherent sample state), and one narrow finishing skill chosen from the actual need. Never stack several taste skills; a shader or image tool must solve a named problem.
  **Why** Taste skills conflict and overdesign.
  **Applies to** process. **Check** n/a (process). **Source** `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s2; `06-design-generation-runbook.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-33]** [team research] Before styling write one line each: product and category; audience; expensive or frustrating moment; product mechanism; single visitor action; proof artifact visible in the frame; three brand traits and one useful contradiction; one visual behaviour native to the product world. If the answers could describe an unrelated startup unchanged, the subject is not specific enough.
  **Why** A hero should not be transplantable.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `DIRECTION.md` has all eight lines. **Source** `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s3; `03-x-saas-hero-spec.md` "Subject definition".

- **RULE [LHS-34]** [team research] Privately sketch three different dominant geometries before coding; they must change the spatial idea, not palette, corner radius or card order. Do not default to left copy + right dashboard, giant headline above a generated photograph, a photograph with one floating UI panel, or a decorative card collage. Structures that count as different: type integrated into a product canvas; one edge-to-edge workflow with copy in its controls; a split where the split is a real comparison/state transition; an editorial composition around one physical artifact; a modular identity system whose repeated units encode product behaviour; a cropped application world with nav and CTA in the same plane.
  **Why** "Three directions are not different if all three keep the same DOM skeleton."
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** three structure names recorded; none equals a rejected skeleton. **Source** `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s4; `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Required pre-build exploration".

- **RULE [LHS-35]** [team research] Give the fictional subject its own wordmark or mark; choose typography from the brand premise not from the agent's usual stack; use sentence case unless the subject voice supports another; start headline exploration between 56 and 84 px at 1920 px and exceed that band only when the composition clearly benefits; treat mono and uppercase utility text as real data language.
  **Why** Prevents the shared grammar; band keeps the rest of the composition alive.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills (1920 canvas). **Check** `font-size` of H1 between 56 and 84 px at 1920; font-family differs from last 8 ledger entries. **Source** `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s5; `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Typography".

- **RULE [LHS-36]** [team research] Do not default to Geist, Inter, Manrope, Space Grotesk or a similar neutral grotesk for every fictional company; record why the display face belongs to the product.
  **Why** Seven r5 concepts all used Geist for nav/body/UI and five also for the H1.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** font-family list vs the five named families. **Source** `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Typography"; `inbox/2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-37]** [team research] Define colour as functional roles: field, ink, secondary surface, signal, state and material colour. Use contrast to control attention. Avoid forcing Larpo monochrome on a fictional subject and avoid the dark-plus-neon and warm-cream-plus-serif clusters unless the subject makes them unavoidable and the composition is otherwise original.
  **Why** Trend clusters read as generated.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** palette roles listed; hue of leading accent differs from other batch entries. **Source** `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s5; `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Color".

- **RULE [LHS-38]** [team research] Navigation: scan in one second (logo, three or four real destinations, sign-in or equivalent, one clear main action when appropriate); normal capitalisation and readable size; avoid the floating pill dock, nested rounded group and excessive empty space; it may be quiet but must feel designed (alignment, rhythm, hover, mark, CTA share one identity). CTA: one dominant by default, written from the visitor's point of view, add a secondary only when it resolves a distinct visitor question; check default, hover, focus, pressed without letting button work consume a screenshot brief.
  **Why** Founder rejected the pill dock and generic CTAs (LHS-04, LHS-05).
  **Applies to** hero concept stills, landing page. **Check** nav links 3-4; primary CTA count 1. **Source** `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Navigation", "CTAs"; `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s6.

- **RULE [LHS-39]** [team research] Make the product the proof: show something the user creates, reviews, controls, compares or approves; show a decision or state transition not decorative analytics; label fictional material `sample` or `concept`; keep names, dates, statuses, measurements and totals coherent; never invent customers, endorsements, outcomes or security claims. The visual must answer what changed, what the user controls and what happens next.
  **Why** Product proof is the evidence layer inside an authored brand.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** sum of visible numbers reconciles; `visual`. **Source** `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s7; `03-x-saas-hero-spec.md` "Product proof".

- **RULE [LHS-40]** [team research] A shader, texture or real-time effect is used only when its behaviour expresses the product; provide a still fallback for the screenshot. A custom logo must come from the same premise as the layout; a generic symbol plus wordmark does not complete the identity.
  **Why** Effects and logos must belong to the subject.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`. **Source** `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Visuals and product UI".

- **RULE [LHS-41]** [team research] Render review: inspect at 100 percent (alignment, type, edges, image quality), at 25 percent (feed hierarchy, CTA clarity), in grayscale (focal order), and in a centre crop plus a modest top/bottom crop (platform resilience).
  **Why** Hero stills are consumed in feeds.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** screenshots at 100%, 25%, grayscale, crops exist in `reports/`. **Source** `03-x-saas-hero-spec.md` "Render review".

- **RULE [LHS-42]** [team research] Internal critique questions: could an unrelated startup use this skeleton by changing logo and copy? is the biggest element also the most meaningful? one idea or several disconnected effects? does the UI prove a workflow? are nav and CTA intentionally designed? does the font choice express the subject? did generated imagery become the concept? does it hold at 25 percent? did the work stay in scope? Any "yes" to the first or seventh, or "no" to the fourth through eighth, requires another pass; change structure before polishing the surface.
  **Why** Codified in the runbook.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`; transplant test (LAI-34). **Source** `06-design-generation-runbook.md` "Internal critique"; `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s8.

- **RULE [LHS-43]** [team research] After delivery append direct acceptance/rejection to the feedback ledger with the exact reason; do not call a rejected result a benchmark; preserve rejected artifacts as labelled negative evidence; do not silently promote a generated concept into canonical guidance; record exactly what was accepted without declaring the whole layout a template.
  **Why** Ledger is system memory.
  **Applies to** process. **Check** ledger entry exists for each verdict. **Source** `06-design-generation-runbook.md` "Knowledge capture after feedback"; `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` s9.

- **RULE [LHS-44]** [team research] The playbook may alone: define a fictional concept, create its brand and interface, generate supporting assets, implement one desktop hero, render and critique. It must escalate: publishing/posting the result; presenting fictional material as client work; adding invented customers, endorsements, outcomes or security claims; changing Larpo's own brand system.
  **Why** Playbook escalation rule (active).
  **Applies to** process. **Check** n/a (process). **Source** `playbooks/x-saas-hero-generation.md` "What this playbook may do alone".

### 2c. Hero quality bar

- **RULE [LHS-45]** [team research] Acceptance at feed scale: at 25 percent size a viewer must identify (1) the company or product identity, (2) the main promise or product moment, (3) the focal visual, (4) the action if there is one. Reject if it becomes a gray block, a giant headline plus tiny UI, or a generic app screenshot.
  **Why** Quality bar, 05.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** downscale to 25% and read the four items. **Source** `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Acceptance at feed scale".

- **RULE [LHS-46]** [team research] Utility mono and uppercase labels only for authentic data or machine language; use sentence case unless the product's voice justifies another choice; tune width, weight, line height and measure together; "a large title is not a substitute for a visual premise."
  **Why** Decorative seriousness is a tell.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** uppercase/mono text count with non-data content. **Source** `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Typography".

- **RULE [LHS-47]** [team research] Start from a proof artifact (what a user creates, reviews, controls, compares or approves); generated imagery must leave room for the intended layout and share lighting, crop and visual grammar.
  **Why** Prevents image-as-concept.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `visual`. **Source** `05-hero-quality-bar.md` "Visuals and product UI".

### 2d. Hero archetype system (for real pages and stills)

- **RULE [LHS-50]** [team research] A hero is the first compressed argument and must answer: is this for me? what changes for me? why should I believe it? what should I do next?
  **Why** Defines the hero's job.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** `visual`. **Source** `inbox/design-intelligence-2026-08-28/02-hero-section-system.md` "The hero's job".

- **RULE [LHS-51]** [team research] Do not polish a hero until you know: one primary audience, one primary action, the exact offer, visitor current vs desired state, strongest proof available now, main objection, traffic context and awareness, real assets, technical constraints (performance, CMS, framework, localisation, motion, accessibility).
  **Why** Polishing without these is premature.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** brief fields filled in `DIRECTION.md`. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Inputs required before design".

- **RULE [LHS-52]** [team research] Use only hero elements that carry a job (context cue, headline, support, action, proof, visual evidence, orientation); every extra item taxes comprehension; a decorative badge is not context; a row of logos is not proof unless true and relevant.
  **Why** Minimal truthful argument.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** list element jobs; remove any without one. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Hero content model"; `inbox/anti-ai-design-playbook-2026-08-28/02-perception-and-hero-psychology.md`.

- **RULE [LHS-53]** [team research] Pick one of 12 archetypes by scoring each candidate 1-5 on content fit, proof strength, brand fit, originality opportunity, mobile resilience, performance cost, implementation risk, accessibility and usefulness without motion; do not choose the most spectacular. Archetypes: 1 product proof, 2 transformation, 3 editorial statement, 4 human outcome, 5 artifact stage, 6 diagrammatic explanation, 7 kinetic narrative, 8 immersive world, 9 evidence first, 10 interactive utility, 11 catalog or collection, 12 quiet confidence.
  **Why** Choose the strongest combined case.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** score table in `DIRECTION.md`. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Twelve hero archetypes", "Choosing the archetype".

- **RULE [LHS-54]** [team research] Create different directions by changing structural variables: centred/split/asymmetric/edge-anchored/layered/radial/full-bleed; giant/medium/compact headline; dense/spacious rhythm; contained artifact vs bleed-through environment; reading path; static/responsive/stateful/narrative visual; direct/delayed/integrated proof. Three directions are not different if they keep the same DOM skeleton.
  **Why** Structure, not colour.
  **Applies to** all. **Check** compare DOM skeletons of directions. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Composition variables".

- **RULE [LHS-55]** [team research] Hero copy: lead with a defensible outcome, tension, distinctive mechanism or category claim; concrete nouns and verbs; remove throat-clearing and empty intensifiers; support line meaningfully different from the headline; CTA matches the commitment (view work, see the demo, start a trial, book a call, compare plans); no secondary CTA unless it serves a different visitor state; exact product language and truthful numbers; read aloud and rewrite if it sounds like a hundred other SaaS pages.
  **Why** Copy rules, 02.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** copy lint (`tools/thresholds.json` banned phrases). **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Copy rules".

- **RULE [LHS-56]** [team research] The hero visual must do at least one of: demonstrate the mechanism, make the result tangible, show scale or detail, establish the brand world, embody the audience, explain the system, create a memorable contrast with the category. If it can be removed without losing meaning or identity it is decoration and must not consume the largest area and performance budget.
  **Why** Visual evidence rules.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** removal test. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Visual evidence rules".

- **RULE [LHS-57]** [team research] Do not shrink the desktop hero into mobile; recompose: preserve message and dominant move, reorder proof and visual evidence by mobile reading priority, collapse complex spatial scenes into one frame or controlled interaction, keep the primary action visible without forcing every element above the fold, test at 320, 375, tablet, small laptop, wide desktop, and make sure decorative canvases cannot cover content or trap input.
  **Why** Mobile is a new composition (not applicable to the single-desktop-still contract, LHS-13).
  **Applies to** landing page, mobile. **Check** screenshots at 320 and 375. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Responsive behavior".

- **RULE [LHS-58]** [team research] Classify hero motion before building: essential (explains the product or interaction continuity), supportive (guides reading), ambient (atmosphere only). Implement in that order; ambient must pause offscreen, degrade cleanly and vanish under reduced motion without leaving an empty composition.
  **Why** Motion budget.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** motion list classified in `DIRECTION.md`. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Motion budget".

- **RULE [LHS-59]** [team research] Score every hero 0-2 on 15 items: audience recognition, promise clarity, mechanism specificity, proof credibility, action clarity, visual hierarchy, brand distinctiveness, composition originality, typography quality, mobile resilience, accessibility, performance discipline, truthfulness, static-frame usefulness, continuity into the next section. A hero below 22/30 does not move to final polish; fix the weakest strategic or structural dimension first.
  **Why** Hero scorecard.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** scorecard total >= 22. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Hero review scorecard".

- **RULE [LHS-60]** [team research] Hero failure patterns to check: headline about the company rather than the visitor; product screenshot too small and generic; decorative motion before content is legible; visual and copy tell unrelated stories; three primary actions; proof delayed far below the first claim; desktop depends on empty width and collapses into a long mobile stack; hero height hard-coded to one viewport and clipping on real devices; effect chosen before concept; borrowing one reference's layout too literally; next section repeats the hero.
  **Why** 02 failure list.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** `visual` plus 100vh clip test at 390x844. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "Failure patterns".

- **RULE [LHS-61]** [team research] A hero is ready to build only with: exact copy; source and licence for every asset; archetype and visual thesis; desktop and mobile composition; states and interaction behaviour; motion classification and reduced-motion behaviour; component and library choices; performance risks and fallback; proof of originality relative to references; acceptance criteria for rendered QA.
  **Why** Handoff contract.
  **Applies to** landing page. **Check** `DIRECTION.md` completeness. **Source** `02-hero-section-system.md` "The handoff contract".

### 2e. Divergence contract (2026-09-01, research capture not yet canonical)

- **RULE [LHS-70]** [team research] Choose the hero class before layout exploration: product-proof (visitor understands input, action, transformation, output or controlled decision from real or believable interface evidence) or brand-campaign (establishes desire, worldview, category position; must not masquerade as product proof). Many weak concepts mix the two: a campaign image presented as software proof plus a tiny status card.
  **Why** Mixed classes make the frame lie about what it proves.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills, landing page. **Check** `DIRECTION.md` names the class. **Source** `inbox/2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Separate two valid hero classes".

- **RULE [LHS-71]** [team research] Collect a reference triad per concept: one structural, one typography/identity, one product-proof/material/motion reference; extract one rule from each; recombine around the product mechanism; do not copy a complete page. Sources by need: SiteInspire Unusual Layout (structure), A1 Editorial / Typographic, Landing.Gallery (offer clarity), SaaSFrame (product storytelling), Awwwards hero collection (signature behaviour + still fallback), Fonts In Use (type in context).
  **Why** Separating sources reduces unconscious copying.
  **Applies to** all. **Check** triad with URLs and one extracted rule each. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Browse by design problem".

- **RULE [LHS-72]** [team research] Before coding a batch fill one row per concept across eight axes: hero class; dominant structure; type voice; palette temperature; CTA grammar; proof mode; logo mechanism; signature behaviour. No two rows may match on more than two of the first six axes.
  **Why** Hard divergence contract.
  **Applies to** batches. **Check** matrix pairwise overlap <= 2 across axes 1-6. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "A hard divergence contract for batches".

- **RULE [LHS-73]** [team research] Reject a batch before export when: more than two concepts share a headline family category; more than two share a header architecture; more than two share a CTA silhouette; an accent hue leads in more than two; a proof card could be moved to another concept unchanged; a logo cannot be explained through the product mechanism in one sentence; three screenshots stay visually similar after grayscale + 24 px blur; the same headline, page edge, corner radius and copy measure appear together twice; the main visual is a generated image with an unrelated software card on top; replacing the company name leaves the identity equally plausible.
  **Why** Macro sameness is invisible to per-element critique.
  **Applies to** batches. **Check** matrix + contact-sheet check (LHS-74). **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Batch rejection rules".

- **RULE [LHS-74]** [team research] Screenshot similarity check for a batch: export every piece at 1440 x 890; make a contact sheet; make grayscale and 24 px Gaussian-blur versions; compare silhouette, value distribution, dominant edge and focal location; reject concepts whose blurred compositions are hard to tell apart; inspect each accepted design at 25 percent.
  **Why** Catches macro sameness.
  **Applies to** batches. **Check** script producing contact sheet + blur variants. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Screenshot similarity check".

- **RULE [LHS-75]** [team research] Use no more than two concepts from one type-voice category (utilitarian grotesk, humanist sans, neo-grotesk, editorial serif, slab/industrial, condensed/poster, mono plus sans, variable display) and do not use Geist as the universal functional layer. Before choosing, render each candidate with the actual wordmark, nav labels, headline with intended line breaks, supporting paragraph, CTA, one product state, numerals, punctuation, arrows and diacritics; use the real italic; use optical sizing when available; set headline by line shape and proof balance, not a fixed size.
  **Why** Common families are not forbidden; repeated unconsidered composition is.
  **Applies to** batches, hero concept stills. **Check** font-category count per batch <= 2. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Typography: use a voice matrix".

- **RULE [LHS-76]** [team research] Define per hero: edge rhythm, reading rhythm, object rhythm, one contrast-jump gap separating major semantic groups, and optical corrections (round marks, diagonals, caps, overhangs, uneven image weight). Do not carry the same 48 px edge, 24-30 px headline gap and 13 px button radius into every identity. An 8 px base is an implementation scaffold, not an instruction to make every hero airy.
  **Why** A dense instrument panel, editorial page, poster and calm care product should not share one rhythm.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** compare edge padding and radius vs last 8 ledger entries. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Spacing".

- **RULE [LHS-77]** [team research] Choose colour in order: category expectation to honour or oppose; emotional temperature; material and imagery world; semantic interface roles; cultural context; contrast and accessibility; repeatability across surfaces and states. Define roles before hex values: canvas, surface, raised/active surface, primary ink, muted ink, action, action hover/pressed, informational signal, positive/warning/critical, material or campaign colour. Tune ramps in OKLCH; verify real fg/bg pairs. Reject one-line colour claims ("blue means trust").
  **Why** Colour associations depend on culture, category, saturation, value, surroundings.
  **Applies to** all. **Check** role table in `DIRECTION.md`; contrast of real pairs. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Color".

- **RULE [LHS-78]** [team research] Derive the logo from the product verb: state the mechanism in one sentence; extract an active verb (connect, compress, reveal, route, compare, approve, protect, sequence, translate, accumulate); choose a geometric behaviour that can demonstrate it; draw silhouette before detail; test one colour, reversed, 16 px, 24 px, 32 px; extend the behaviour into crop, layout, icons, product proof and motion; customise the wordmark only where the edit reinforces it.
  **Why** One product idea should govern multiple brand components (TwelveLabs, Intrinsic, Dashlane, Satalia examples).
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** logo renders legibly at 16/24/32 px; one-sentence rationale. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Logo and identity".

- **RULE [LHS-79]** [team research] Pick one signature behaviour, not a stack of grain, glow, parallax, glass, particles, cursor trails and procedural backgrounds. Map product behaviour to visual behaviour: detection -> scan/focus/isolate/reveal; compression -> converge/fold/reduce; collaboration -> converge/hand off/synchronise; versioning -> layer/scrub/compare/branch; routing -> trace/flow/arrive; forecasting -> echo/project/fan out/resolve; privacy -> mask/contain/locally reveal; generation -> assemble from constrained parts then settle. For screenshot-first work the still state must carry the idea.
  **Why** Motion supports comprehension and continuity, not decoration.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** `DIRECTION.md` names one behaviour and its product verb. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Motion, shaders, and effects".

- **RULE [LHS-80]** [team research] Acceptance for the next seven concepts: seven different fictional names and mechanisms; at least five clearly different macro silhouettes; at least five different type voices; no more than two centred three-part navigation bars; no more than two filled rounded CTA silhouettes; no repeated coral/cobalt/cream combination; at least three product-proof heroes and at least two brand-campaign heroes; every logo explains and extends a mechanism; no floating proof card unless it is the real product artifact; each reads differently in a blurred grayscale contact sheet; each legible at 25 percent; each records its reference triad and what was borrowed as a rule.
  **Why** Written acceptance criteria for the batch.
  **Applies to** batches of 7. **Check** matrix + contact sheet. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Acceptance criteria for the next seven concepts".

- **RULE [LHS-81]** [team research] Do not generate another batch directly from research; first create or approve five narrow internal skills (reference-triangulation, hero-divergence-gate, brand-mark-from-mechanism, typography-role-sampler, visual-similarity-qa) and use them to prepare the divergence matrix. Do not install an external "anti-slop" repository merely because of its name; the missing capabilities are comparison, enforced divergence, reference extraction and visual evaluation.
  **Why** More taste prompts do not fix batch similarity.
  **Applies to** process. **Check** n/a (process). **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Skill and repository review", "Recommended next action".

- **RULE [LHS-82]** [team research] Avoid the learned convergent grammar measured in the r5 batch: neutral grotesk; tightly tracked 62-83 px headline; 46-52 px page edge; logo left / three links near centre / action right; rounded filled CTA with arrow; one large image plus a floating proof/status object; cream, coral, cobalt, amber or dark photographic colour. Changing only the photograph or which side the copy occupies is not divergence.
  **Why** The measured sameness across seven concepts.
  **Applies to** hero concept stills. **Check** compare H1 px, edge padding, nav architecture, CTA radius with batch. **Source** `2026-09-01-hero-divergence-and-design-research.md` "Evidence from the latest seven-concept batch".
