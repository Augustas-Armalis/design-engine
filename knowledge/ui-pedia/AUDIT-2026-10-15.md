# UI Pedia gap audit (2026-10-15)

Founder's question: "have you really read absolutely everything, from spacing to buttons to each little nifty thing and rule, fully?"

Short answer: **not before today.** The first study captured the book's *text* almost completely. It missed a lot of what lives only in the *figures*: measured proportions, state renderings, layout grammar and small spacing relationships. It also had about 20 wrong figure descriptions. A parser bug kept 28 written rules out of the index. All three are fixed now. Every page of the 14 visual-craft chapters has been viewed as an image. The other 12 chapters got a lighter pass (all text read, images viewed wherever the text pointed to a figure or the OCR was thin).

Freebie books: see [`../FREEBIES-COVERAGE-2026-10-15.md`](../FREEBIES-COVERAGE-2026-10-15.md).

## Method

- One auditor per chapter or chapter group (13 in parallel), all working from one brief. For each page they read the OCR text **and** viewed the page image (`corpus/ui-pedia/pages/p-NNNN.jpg`, or a 144 dpi PDF render for measurements). They listed every concrete teachable point and compared it to the note at the same level of specificity.
- Nothing was deleted or reworded. Missing rules sit under `### Audit additions (audit 2026-10-15)` at the end of each note's section 2. Missing numbers and do/don't pairs were appended to sections 3 and 4. Coverage, corrections and open points are in section 7. Every addition carries `(audit 2026-10-15)`.
- Clear factual errors were fixed in place and logged in section 7 as `corrected: ... was ..., page shows ...`.
- **Measured values are page-scale proportions, not CSS px.** UI Pedia almost never prints pixel values; it draws them. Audit numbers taken from figures are labelled "measured" in the notes. Use them as ratios (for example a between-group gap of 2.5x the within-group gap), not as specs.
- This file was compiled from the audit trail each auditor wrote into its note (the section 2 additions and the section 7 coverage lines), not from their chat reports. Rule counts come from the `tools/build_index.py` opener regex, run against `git HEAD` (before) and the working tree (after).

## Index format fix (all chapters)

28 rules were written as `**RULE (qualifier)**`: 19 in ch14 (the weight factors and the shape meanings), 7 in 25a, 1 in 05 and 1 in 06. The opener regex in `build_index.py` does not accept that form, so these rules **never reached `system/rules/index.json`**. They are now normalised to `**RULE** <text> (qualifier)` with the wording unchanged, and the fix is logged in each note's section 7. The same bug hides 49 rules in `knowledge/web/typography.md`, which is outside this audit and left for later.

## What was missing, by chapter

**02 Color.** Figure-only recipes: the exact shade and tint mix steps (17/34/51/63/75% toward black or white); hue variants in 5-degree steps up to +/-15 degrees; the UI base colour as a tint of the saturated hue (#758BFD); hover/pressed about 30 brightness points darker at the same hue; disabled and loading renderings; button shadows tinted with the fill hue; dark labels on yellow fills; status alerts that colour only the icon disc, tint and border; non-text contrast. Resolved: the p.62 tone row is not a linear grey mix; the WebAIM foreground is #9095FE.

**04 Alignment.** Align the internal division lines across sibling cards, not just the outer edges; equal top padding inside unequal containers; numeric column headers aligned to the value edge; currency once in the header; fixed decimals; summary rows right-aligned against the values; a logo subline on the logo's anchor edge; icons in media objects centred on their text block; a raised featured card stays in its column band. Two figure descriptions corrected (p.96 line count, p.100 header alignment).

**05 Proportion.** The 7-rule count was mostly honest: the chapter is a single system (the golden ratio), and five of its 11 pages are history or construction. The figures still held three rules: the hero copy block takes the 61.8% square; the header band comes from the smallest golden cell; subdivide only the sidebar, never the main square.

**06 Whitespace.** The text has no px values; the figures do. Buy whitespace by shrinking the content, not by enlarging the card. Inside a card, the gap under the image equals the side padding (about 28 px drawn). Gaps between stacked elements are about 2x the line gap inside an element. A horizontal media card gives the image about 43% of the width. Single-task pages get extensive macro whitespace. Corrected: the p.123 "indentation" claim is not supported by the figure.

**07 Proximity.** The figures use between-group gaps of **2.5x to 7x** the within-group gap. The engine note's 1.5-2x was too low, so the measured floor of about 2.5x is now recorded. Spacing, not shape or colour, defines group membership. In text, gaps step up in a fixed order: line < heading-to-paragraph <= paragraph-to-paragraph < space above the next heading.

**10 Icons.** Keyshapes are sized to equal area (circle diameter about 1.11x the square side); open glyphs that fill the circle are scaled down; a play triangle is centred by its centroid; icons sit in fixed-width slots so labels share one edge; the icon-to-label gap is about 1.0-1.2x the icon width (measured); test sheets are built at three sizes on two tints. Corrected: the test sheets are on two *light* tints, not light and dark; 65 arrows, not 66; 21 Apple icons, not 20.

**11 Layout and grids.** The outer margin is wider than the gutter, and the gutter clearly narrower than a column; modular grids use one gutter on both axes with square modules; the column count steps 2 -> 4 -> 8 -> 12 with the viewport; breakpoints change what is shown, not only its size; cards switch from image-beside-text to image-above-text when they narrow; feature-plus-stack in a 2/3 + 1/3 split; masonry keeps its outer edges flush; nested frames can carry their own grids. **Five figure-count corrections** (p.223 8 columns and 4 rows, p.226 9 columns and 8 gutters, p.227 9 and 4 columns): the first study had estimated these by eye.

**12 Typography.** Below the heading levels, small roles share one size and differ by weight; headings differ in colour as well as size; a novelty or display face is supported by a plain sans; "02/04" counters emphasise the current number and mute the total; the card type stack (muted category label > title > body); validation adds only a green check inside a valid field. Corrected: Subtitle, Body 1 and Button are drawn at about the same size, so the scale does not keep decreasing.

**13 Buttons (the founder's main concern).** 19 additions: vertical padding is measured to cap height, not to the line box; the icon goes before the label; a six-property build spec; a safe-area zone around each button; hover = the same hue darker; one height across every variant in an action group; secondary and primary grouped at one edge, with tertiary or destructive at the opposite edge; full-width primary with a centred text Cancel in stacked forms; two text buttons told apart only by the accent colour; links look like underlined links; toggle and segmented selection uses a tinted fill; dropdown panels match the trigger width; nav and hero CTAs share one style; FAB ripple and lift; the expandable + -> x morph; the two-segment animated delete button; swipe actions as tinted square tiles; an icon button fills its touch target; filled and outline variants share radius and size, with a 1px brand-colour outline. Corrected: the p.317 shape has two diagonally rounded corners, not a cut corner.

**14 Objects and shapes.** 19 written rules were invisible to the index (format fix). New: mixed corner radii paired diagonally (60/02); a pill radius equals half the short side; a thin concentric accent ring with a gap isolates circular avatars and play buttons; flush media takes the card radius on its touching corners; one shared slant for every diagonal element; triangle mosaics alternate orientation; thumbnail rows keep one size and radius; shapes are drawn as vectors; a short hairline divides name from role. Corrected: the p.354 mosaic alternates down/up/down.

**16 Forms.** The label sits above the field, flush left, and closer to its own field than to the previous one; the primary button matches the field width and is about 0.85x the field height; the validation icon sits inside the trailing edge at about 0.4x the field height; the error banner names the fields and the cause; the placeholder states the requirement; multi-step screens use a "Step N" eyebrow and a question heading; two step-navigation grammars; the stepper node and line spec; hairline group dividers; trailing affordance icons by field type; the primary label echoes the step outcome. Corrected: progress-bar fills of about 35% and 62%; the Vs figure is on p.421.

**17 Navigation.** The first study had treated much of this chapter as prose. New: the where-am-I / where-can-I-go test; deliberate action order; the desktop top-bar grammar (wordmark left, 4 links grouped, CTA right); explicitly designed collapsed states; the active item marked by a shape cue plus the accent colour; chevrons only on items with a submenu; menu panels attached to their trigger; the mega-menu chip-group spec; the 3-zone sidebar; chevrons on parent items only; breadcrumbs broadest to current with spaced slashes; the 2-tier commerce header; non-pattern cues (category tag, author, related). The book shows no hover, focus or disabled nav states; that is recorded as a gap.

**20 Cards.** 15 additions, all figure-borne: media inset by one equal gutter; two hugging actions at the bottom on the text edge; three visible tiers when there are more than two actions; the price typography "From $2 / Person"; paired selectors in one split bar; a consistent dashboard-card header; masonry with an identical width and gutter; zero-gap mosaics rounded only on the outer corners; carousel arrows outside the deck; a hover pop-up card that grows over its neighbours; horizontal listing and kanban-status cards; hover-reveal action rows; a table fallback with colour-coded row actions.

**23 Shadows and blurs.** The shadow form follows its job (a light white separator vs. a dark lift); inset shadows land on the edges opposite their offset (X -7, Y -8, Blur 13); stacked hard shadows step evenly (Y 4/8/12); higher layers get lighter as well as shadowed; one fixed depth plane per surface (background, content, overlay); the glass card recipe; the material gradient's light stop at the light-source corner (#758EFE). Corrected: the inner shadow sits on the bottom and right inner edges, not the top-left.

**Lighter-pass chapters.** Mostly small figure-borne items: equal-area geometry for shape sets and identical paired buttons (03); checkout stepper node states, the payment segmented tab, add-to-cart double feedback, the social sign-in order, and no prefer-the-company emphasis on consent choices (08); multi-attribute focal contrast and between-group gaps several times the within-group gap (09); column drag, resize, filter-row and row-detail specs (15); a single travelling tab indicator and interpolated switch states (18); illustration for attention and narrative, real product UI inside illustrations, mascot re-staging, the mascot as a separate brand asset (19); side-dependent thumb zones (21); gradient direction and confinement in product UI (22); type styling over typeface choice, free stand-in fonts, critique of self-study (25a); storyboards and flows first, responsiveness as a UX defect, the no-results search state (01). 24 and 25b had nothing to add.

**Overall:** before the audit, the engine had roughly 75% of UI Pedia's teachable content, and roughly 70% of it was reachable through the index. The text was close to fully read. The images were not. After the audit, every page of the 14 visual-craft chapters has been checked as text and image, and every page across all 26 chapters is accounted for in a section 7 coverage line.

**Residual risks:**
- (a) Most measured values are page-scale proportions. Use them as ratios, not as specs.
- (b) In the lighter-pass chapters, prose pages were read only as OCR text. Those pages are listed in each note's section 7.
- (c) Several book figures contradict their own text (gradient reflected/linear, p.227 fluid margins, the p.249 5-block row, the p.338 coordinate label). These are flagged in section 7, not resolved.
- (d) `knowledge/web/typography.md` still has 49 rules the index cannot see.
