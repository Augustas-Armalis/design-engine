# Logo reference credits

Study evidence only. Every mark here belongs to its designer or brand owner. These screenshots exist so the engine can learn construction (counts, angles, stroke/gap ratios, pairing). Never copy, trace, adapt or present any of them as engine or Larpo work. See `knowledge/web/logo-craft.md` for what was learned.

Captured 2026-10-09 with headless Chromium (Playwright). No account was used, no cookies accepted beyond "Reject All", nothing was liked, saved, followed or posted. Pinterest search (`pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=abstract logo mark`) showed a login wall, so per instructions it was not used; galleries below replaced it.

Sources:
- **Logosystem** (logosystem.co): curated logo gallery; each card credits a designer and links to the original post. Screenshots are of the gallery image element on the logo's detail page (2x, downscaled to 1000px).
- **SVGL** (svgl.app, open-source SVG logo library by pheralb): shipped tech brand marks, rendered at 440px on white from the library SVG URL, and 67 wordmark lockups (`lockups/`).
- **Dribbble** tag page `dribbble.com/tags/logo-construction` (one page screenshot in `sheets/`, cookies rejected) as evidence of the construction-grid presentation genre.

Other files in this folder (`marks/`, `nav/`, `renders/`, `nav-pairing.json`, `marks-sheet.png`, `renders-sheet.png`) come from a separate study and are credited there.

## Files

- `measurements.json`: pixel measurements of the marks and lockups (method in its `_about`); `measure-marks.mjs` is the script.
- `scale-test-1x.png`: shipped marks rendered at 16/24/32/64px, flattened to one colour (`brightness(0)`) and inverted, plus a synthetic ring-and-bar stroke ladder (3-16% of mark height). Own test render.
- `sheets/`: contact sheets. `sym-*` pure symbols, `det-*` all 159 captured logosystem detail images, `saas-*` 87 SVGL marks, `lock-*` SVGL lockups, `logosystem-*-fold` category pages, `dribbble-logo-construction`.
