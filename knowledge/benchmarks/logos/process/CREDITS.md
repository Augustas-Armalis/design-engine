# Logo process references: credits

Study evidence only. Every image here belongs to its designer, studio, publisher or brand owner. They are kept so the engine can learn how marks are explored, drawn and refined (sketch volume, route boards, node placement, curve tension, curvature continuity, optical correction, refinement passes). Never copy, trace, adapt or present any of them as engine or Larpo work. What was learned is in `knowledge/web/logo-drawing.md`; the method built from it is `system/logo/drawing-method.md`.

Captured 2026-10-10 with headless Chromium (Playwright, 1440px viewport). Each file is an element screenshot of one image on the public page named below, at its rendered size. No account was used. Cookie banners were declined (the "Reject"/"Decline" button where one existed) or hidden with CSS; nothing was accepted, liked, saved, followed or posted.

## Bezier anatomy of shipped marks

The curve measurements in `knowledge/web/logo-drawing.md` section 2 come from `tools/logo_curves.mjs` run on the 32 SVGs in `../marks/` (sources in `../CREDITS.md` and `knowledge/web/logo-anatomy-measured.md`) and on 61 SVGs from the open-source SVGL library (https://github.com/pheralb/svgl, `static/library/*.svg`, fetched as text and parsed as data, never executed). The SVGL files were analysed in the scratch directory and not stored in the repo; only the numbers are kept, in `curves-measured.json` (own measurement data).
