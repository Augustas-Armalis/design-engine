Generated by `aggregate.py` from `raw/*.json` (written by `measure-live.mjs`). Chromium 156, viewport 1440x900, DPR 2. Pages are public docs/marketing pages of each system; numbers are the rendered result, not the source.

## Files

- `measure-live.mjs <slug> <url>`: icon centring (svg centre vs container border-box centre), ink offset inside svg, parity, text cap-centre vs box centre, control heights, radius, nested-radius audit.
- `measure-glyph-centering.mjs`: raster ink bbox centre and mass centroid of the same glyph in Lucide, Phosphor, Heroicons, Radix Icons (-> `glyph-centering.json`).
- `measure-text-centering.mjs`: pixel-measured cap-height centring of a button label in 18 fonts x 3 sizes, with and without `text-box: trim-both cap alphabetic` (-> `text-centering.json`).
- `measure-centering-lab.mjs`: which CSS recipe centres an icon, incl. odd/even parity (-> `centering-lab.json`).
- `measure-icon-crispness.mjs`: stroke crispness of a 24-grid icon at other sizes, DPR 1 and 2 (-> `icon-crispness.json`).
- `measure-numerals.mjs`: tabular numerals on public pages (-> `raw-numerals/`).
