# Bento benchmarks: credits

Screenshots of public marketing pages. We keep them here only as study references for the engine (see `knowledge/web/bento.md` and `knowledge/web/bento-craft.md`). Every image belongs to the site it came from. None is ours, and none may be used in client work. Captures were taken with Playwright (Chromium, 1440x900, deviceScaleFactor 2) unless marked. Mobile captures were taken at 390x844 @1x. Non-essential cookies were rejected or the banner was hidden. Nothing was accepted, and no capture used a login.
